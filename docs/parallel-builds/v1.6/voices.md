LANE: voices

Goal: characters speak, and impacts sound like what they hit.
Recorded samples now exist in public/audio (see scripts/audio/sounds.mjs): boss voices boss_<king|rex|queen|omega>_<intro|phase|defeat>, boss roars roar_<king|rex|queen|omega>, choir_stab, hero barks bark_<rookie|bulwark|dash|ace>_<start|level|lowhp|bigkill|boss|revive|evolve|win>, material impacts hit_slime, hit_stone, hit_shield (plus existing hit_flesh, hit_metal).
1. Boss voice and vocal hits: each boss plays its intro line at its entrance (systems/bossFight.ts / bossIntro.ts), its phase line + a choir stab + its own roar on phase changes, its defeat line on death. Priorities so the announcer and boss don't talk over each other (route through the announcer's queue or a shared voice channel with ducking).
2. Hero barks: the local hero says short lines on run start, level-up (rarely), low HP (once per dip), big kill/multi-kill, boss appears, revived, evolution, stage win; cooldowns so it's occasional and charming, never spammy; co-op: only your own hero's barks (others' at low volume optional). Setting "Hero voices" (on by default). Skins keep the hero's voice.
3. Material-aware impacts: every enemy type gets a material (metal, flesh/soft, slime, stone, shield) in data; hit sounds pick the material sample, with crits and shield hits distinct.
4. Sound designer tools: a SOUND folder/panel in the dev debug panel (src/ui/debugPanel.ts, opened with `) that lists every sample and cue, plays it, and live-tunes per-sound gain/pitch/variation/cooldown with the values saved to localStorage and an "export JSON" button to paste into a data file (src/audio/soundTuning.ts) that the game reads.
Owns: new src/audio/voices.ts, src/audio/soundTuning.ts, src/ui/debugSound.ts, material field in src/data/enemies.ts (additive), cue mapping in src/audio/mix.ts (additive), small hooks in bossFight/bossIntro/hero events, settings toggle, tests/voices.test.ts. Another lane (audiomix) edits sfx.ts/mix.ts mixing internals — keep your edits additive.
