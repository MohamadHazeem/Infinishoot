Lane g8-rhythm (Gate 8): rhythm hybrid combat and a boss that fights to the music.
Owns: new src/game/beatCombat.ts, a beat accuracy HUD element (new module), the music beat API consumers (src/audio/music.ts read-only plus small accessors), a new boss "Maestro" in src/game/bosses* (additive) with its theme, tests/g8rhythm.test.ts, notes/lanes/g8-rhythm.md.
Build: (1) ON-BEAT combat, always on but subtle: shots, dashes and parries within a tight window of the beat deal more damage or refund stamina, with a readable beat pulse on the crosshair; off by a setting for players who dislike it; (2) Maestro, a boss whose every attack lands on the music's beats and bars, with phases that change tempo; fully telegraphed; appears as a rare route boss; (3) works with music volume at zero (visual beat only) and in co-op (host clock); (4) EN/AR; (5) skilled bot gains measurably from on-beat play (report the damage bonus it earns) and beats Maestro within normal boss times.
