LANE g4-nemesis (Gate 4): adaptive enemies and the nemesis system.
Owns: new src/game/nemesis.ts and src/game/adaptive.ts, a separate normalized store for nemesis history (src/core/storage.ts, own key, no save-format break), the nemesis HUD/intro (new src/ui/nemesisUi.ts), tests/g4nemesis.test.ts, notes/lanes/g4-nemesis.md.
Build: (1) adaptive resistance: if one damage type is over ~60% of a team's damage for 2+ minutes, new spawns gain a gradual resistance to it (capped, shown with a visible resist badge and an announcer line), decaying when you diversify; (2) nemesis: the enemy that kills you is promoted, gets a generated name (EN/AR), a scar, one new trait, and appears in later runs with an intro card; beating it drops a unique relic and ends its story; history kept per profile; (3) co-op: the host decides; the nemesis is the one that downed the most players; (4) readable, fair, capped (never more than one nemesis per stage); (5) report how often a nemesis appears and its win rate against the skilled bot.
