Owns: the four loop-1 bosses (King Bopper, Rampage Rex, Hive Queen, Omega Overlord) in src/game/bosses.ts and their tuning, the mini-boss move sets (src/game/bosses* minis), tests/g4bosses.test.ts, notes/lanes/g4-bosses.md. Do NOT touch Gate 2's soft-enrage code beyond calling it.
Build: (1) each boss gets at least 3 mechanics a player must learn (readable tells, a fixed choreography per phase with small variations); (2) one mechanic per boss that is not damage (a puzzle such as hitting switches, an arena shift, positioning or a shield that only a parry breaks); (3) a phase-3 desperation act with its own music cue and banner; (4) minis get their own move sets instead of shrunken copies; (5) co-op and online sync through the existing boss snapshot `bx`; (6) exit criterion: the skilled bot's first-try win rate per boss is under 50% and rises clearly over 5 practice runs (simulate learning by giving the bot boss-specific knowledge flags); report per boss.
