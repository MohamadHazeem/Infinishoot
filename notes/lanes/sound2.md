# Lane: sound, wave 2 (duel music, crowd, radio DJ, instruments, ears, surround, feature sounds)

Everything is synthesized with WebAudio on the existing engine (no new recordings, no dependencies, no new assets). Sound stays local to each machine; the only gameplay parts (instrument pickups, sound-reactive enemies) run on the host and reach clients through one new snapshot field.

## What players get
- **Boss music duel.** In a stage boss's last phase (phase 3 for King, Rex and Queen and Omega, the Titan's exposed core) and while the ring-of-fire duel (`game.bossDuel`) runs, the boss theme turns into call and response in 2-bar phrases. The boss's phrase: its lead plays louder and is doubled an octave down on a gritty saw, your weapon stems drop to 25%. Your phrase: the boss lead and counter drop out, the arp thins, your weapon stems play at 2.4x, and a square-wave lead answers the boss's last phrase (same rhythm, a third up). Your kills and the damage you deal the boss during your phrase add accents: bell stabs on chord tones, and a louder answer. A small tag under the boss bar says BOSS'S TURN / YOUR TURN! HIT IT! (only while the music actually plays). Tempo and the beat clock never change (the MODES lane's rhythm stage reads them).
- **Comeback music.** While this machine's hero is in comeback mode (`hero.comeback`), the song turns minor (major 3rd, 6th and 7th flattened), a 1.5 kHz low-pass closes over it and a heartbeat kick pulses on beats 1 and 1-and. It changes at the next bar; the filter moves at once. Healing brings the normal song back. Plus a low brass hit and heartbeat (`s2:comeback`) when it starts (it replaces the placeholder powerUp).
- **The arena crowd** (SETTINGS > CROWD, on by default). An invisible crowd: a bed of three bands of looped noise (a low murmur panned left and right, a brighter chatter) that swells with the fight (combo, a boss, the duel, your style rank), and reactions: cheers (a noise roar plus 4-7 rising "yay" formant voices spread across the stereo field, a whistle on big ones), roars (long roar, chorus, applause, two whistles), applause, whistles, "oohs", gasps, boos (low, falling "uuu") and an "aww". When: a boss arrives (ooh), a boss phase (roar), a boss dies (roar), combo 50 / 100 / every 100 after (cheer, a roar from 200), 25+ team kills in a second (cheer), a stage clear (applause), an instrument joins the band (cheer), a near miss (the time lane's bullet time: gasp), a perfect dodge (gasp), a parry (ooh), a last stand opening (gasp), won (roar) or lost (aww), a clutch revive (cheer), a single hit of 15%+ of your life (boo), going down (aww). Every reaction has its own cooldown (2.2-6 s) and any two are 0.6 s apart. The crowd is on the effects bus (so the announcer duck applies) and also dips to 35% whenever anyone talks (barks, the announcer, boss lines, the DJ).
- **Radio DJ** (SETTINGS > RADIO DJ, on by default). After a stage clear, once the announcer and barks are done and you're between stages (stage clear, portal, transition, shop, route map), DJ VOLT on RADIO INFINI 88.8 does a short break: a station jingle, an intro, the two best highlights of the stage, a sign-off, a closing sting (about 4-6 s). Lines come from the stage's highlights with your numbers: "Zero hits on Rex? Respect!", "Combo of 312, the crowd loves you!", "That Titan never stood a chance!", "The King down in 24 seconds flat!", "A last stand, and you walked right out of it. Unreal!", "2 close calls in one stage. My heart can't take it!", "And is that a cowbell I hear in the mix? Groovy!", a line per world event, the mech, a supply crate, a rewind, a comeback, a flawless stage, co-op rescues, big kill counts and levels (about 25 lines, scored, best two picked). The voice is the formant synth speaking the line's own vowels (each word split into its vowel syllables, pitch declining over the sentence, stressed word starts, a rise on ! and ?), through a radio chain: high-pass 380 Hz, a +7 dB presence peak at 1.7 kHz, soft clipping, low-pass 3.3 kHz, a little static. It waits its turn on the shared voice channel (priority 8: above hero barks, below the announcer and boss lines, which can cut it), pulls the music to 50% while it talks, and every line is captioned (CAPTIONS). An ON AIR panel (bottom left, over the loadout) shows the station, a blinking light and an EQ; with CAPTIONS off it also shows the line, so the gibberish has words.
- **Instrument pickups.** 45% of stages (seeded by run seed and stage, its own RNG so the game RNG is untouched) hide one instrument the band doesn't have yet, appearing 20-65% of the way into the stage, 9-18 m from a hero on walkable ground: a big floating music note in the instrument's colour, spinning and bobbing on the beat over a pulsing ring, plus a locator chime every 2.2 s from where it is (heard up to 45 m, panned), so you can find it by ear. Toast: "SOMETHING MUSICAL IS NEARBY: FOLLOW THE COWBELL!". Walk into it: banner "COWBELL JOINS THE BAND! MORE COWBELL!", a fanfare, and the instrument plays in the run's soundtrack for the rest of the run (stage songs, boss themes, the shop; not the title, jukebox or stingers): Cowbell (offbeat clanks, more in choruses), Sax (a bandpassed saw answering the melody in odd bars), Steel Drum (FM bells, syncopated), Choir (a bandpassed wide pad on the chord), Slap Bass (plucked root plus octave pops). All in the song's key, chord and stage transposition. A BAND strip in the goals stack (top left) shows the collected instruments as 12x12 pixel chips (24 px). Co-op: any hero can grab it; the band is the team's, synced by the host, and every machine's music adds it.
- **Sound-reactive enemies.** Swarmers (ears 1), Bumble Bees (1.2) and Hop Frogs (0.9) have ears (`EnemyDef.ears`). Every gameplay sound leaves a noise blob where it played; nearby sounds merge and stack a little, blobs fade with a 1.1 s time constant. An eared enemy hears the loudest blob within 40 m x ears (linear falloff). Loud (heard >= 0.6) and more than 4.5 m from any hero: it rushes to the noise at 1.25x speed without attacking (an explosion on the far side pulls it away from you). Quiet (heard < 0.3) and no hero within 12 m: it loses track and ambles around where it lost you at 0.4x. Within 4.5 m of a hero it just fights. Tells over them: a bouncing yellow ♪ (drawn in) or a drifting cyan zZ (lost you), within 30 m of your hero. Rapid Buster fire carries about 20 m; a charge shot, the shield, drones and auras don't draw anything.
- **Surround stingers.** Big moments sweep around your head: a boss arrival circles you exactly once (2.6 s, brass swell, rumble, rising whoosh), the Titan rises behind you and goes round 1.5 times (sub swell, grinding, groan), an elite landing swooshes half way round, an eclipse circles slowly with a dark drone, the colossus passes from your left to your right with a boom at the closest point, and a jackpot rains coins around you twice (`s2:jackpot` / `playSurround('jackpot')`, ready for the CINEMATICS lane's jackpot chest). Stereo: the pan follows the angle and a low-pass closes to 1.8 kHz while it's behind you. HEADPHONES 3D AUDIO: a real HRTF panner moves on a circle around the local hero (the listener), oriented by where it faces.
- **Proper sounds for wave-1 features** (they used placeholders): mech suit boot-up (servos spooling, two locking clunks, hydraulic hiss, a ready chirp), servo steps, stomps (step plus shockwave thud and hiss), arm cannons (punchy autocannon with a casing clink), missile salvo (four launches), eject (latches, gas, the empty suit clanking) and a mech drop landing with a beacon; supply crate flare (sputter), a plane droning over, the chute snapping open and fluttering down, a wooden landing thud with rattling contents; the Titan rising (a crack, then rock grinding up for its 3 s rise), arm sweeps (a huge stone whoosh), weak points shattering (glassy crack, ringing shards, a power-down whine); world event start stingers for all eight kinds (a groan and cracks, a distant water roar, a splitting rumble, far footfalls, the light draining away, a sprouting chime, a building rumble that blows, a cricket-and-clock hush), cracking floor, a collapse roaring away into the void, colossus steps (sub thump, rock crunch, debris patter), water rushing in while a flood rises and splashes when you wade, a low uneasy drone through the eclipse, the sun bursting back (a bright chord swell), the flower blooming (a harp run and pollen whoosh), a volcano rumble bed while it erupts; the rewind is now a tape rewinding (transport clunk, motor whine, the last seconds chattering past backwards at speed, hiss); the death replay is a slow-motion whoosh (a reverse-cymbal swell, then a deep dragged-out whoosh and a slurred tape); the last stand gets a heartbeat that speeds up as its 5 s run out (0.85 s -> 0.4 s) and a clock ticking tick-tock every 0.5 s.

## How it works
- `src/game/systems/sound2.ts`: `soundLaneStep` (host/solo, from Game.simulate after the world events: noise decay, instrument roll/spawn/pickup, tells double-buffered from the enemies' decisions), `hear` (EnemyCtx.hear), `soundLaneNet` / `applySoundLaneNet` (snapshot `s2`), `soundLaneFrame` (every machine, from systems/sound.ts updateSound: music switches and accents, crowd, highlights and the DJ, surround on boss arrival, beds, the view and the HUD).
- Audio modules (all new, src/audio/): `musicDuel.ts` (phrase rules, minor scale), `instruments.ts` (defs, patterns, `InstrumentLayers` slots in each song like the weapon stems), `crowd.ts`, `radio.ts` (highlights -> script, text -> syllables, `RadioDj`), `surround.ts` (paths and curves, `playSurround`), `loudness.ts` (table, `NoiseField`, `hearDecision`), `featureCues.ts` (`LANE_CUES` recipes played as `s2:<name>` effects-bus sounds, the mech's weapon cues), `synthGraph.ts` (`LaneVoice`: a self-cleaning tone/noise/vowel voice that implements SynthKit, for graphs with their own chain).
- Music (`music.ts`) additions, no API changes: `setDuel(on)`, `duelAccent(x)`, `duelTurn`, `setTension(on)`, `tense`, `setInstruments(mask)`, `instruments`, `setTalkover(on)`. A new `duel` layer (call/answer/accents, the comeback heartbeat), per-song `InstrumentLayers`, a tense minor copy of the style, and `WeaponStems.commit(..., mul)` for the duel. Worst case measured (Omega, phase 3, six evolved stems, the whole band, the duel, SS rank, 40 s): 70 of 96 music voices, no drops.
- sfx: `SynthKit` gained `swell` (a tone that swells in) and an attack on `noise`; noise longer than 0.9 s loops the buffer. `sfx.named` plays `s2:` cues (`laneCue`), `sfx.weapon` falls through to the lane's weapon cues, `sfx.elite` adds its surround swoosh. `cuePriority` reads lane cue priorities.
- Co-op: the snapshot's `s2` = { b: band mask, d: [kind, x, z] pickup or null, h: [uid, state, ...] tells near that client's hero (max 48) }, null when there's nothing (generic codec path, no codec change; old clients ignore it). All `s2:` cues travel on the effects bus as names. Clients hear their own crowd, DJ, duel and comeback music from synced state (boss phase, `game.bossDuel`, `hero.comeback`, team kills, combo). Old clients get unknown `s2:` names and stay silent for them.

## Unfinished / risky
- **Nobody listened to anything.** Levels are set from rendered peaks against the existing cues, not by ear: the crowd (cheer ~0.16 peak) could be too quiet or too busy, the DJ's gibberish could be charming or grating, the duel's call and answer could be muddy over a dense boss theme. All the gains are in the debug panel.
- **Sound-reactive enemies change balance.** Swarmers are the commonest enemy. Rapid fire (and AUTO-FIRE) pulls them in from ~20 m at 1.25x speed; a quiet build (right click, auras, drones) lets the ones beyond 12 m wander, so fewer reach you (fewer kills and XP, and the director doesn't top up enemies that are merely calm). Not measured with bot runs. Knobs: `hearingTuning` (or `enabled`), and `ears` on the three defs. Eared enemies that are drawn in or calm don't attack until within 4.5 m (the `lure` contract).
- **The duel follows the boss's phase on this machine**; Omega's last phase (25%) and the Titan's core start it. The music duel only covers boss tracks; the Titan uses the stage's boss track.
- **The ON AIR panel and DJ timing** depend on the voice channel: if a boss line or the announcer is speaking when the 10 s window ends, the break is dropped for that stage. The DJ doesn't talk in PvP or on the starting shop.
- **The BAND strip** sits in the goals stack, which is getting tall on busy stages (objective, event, challenge, world event); a first-time tip panel can cover it for a few seconds.
- **The surround stingers' HRTF path** uses the listener's position and facing at the moment it starts; if the hero turns fast the circle stays where it was (it's a world-space path). The elite swoosh fires with every elite arrival (4 s cooldown).
- One full `npm test` run failed tests/worldEventsGame.test.ts's Night Raid "cache taken" check (+0 gold); it passed on the two runs of that file alone and on the next full run (48/48). The cache only checks the hero's position, so I take it for an existing flake (a hunter's knockback pushing the hero off the cache), but swarmers with ears do move differently on that test's raid, so keep an eye on it.
- `npm run coop` (two real instances over the PeerJS broker) wasn't run; co-op is covered by the in-process host/client test.
- Instrument pickups have no minimap dot or edge arrow (the locator chime and the toast are the cue).
