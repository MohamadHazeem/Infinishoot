# Lane: spectate (watch your friends play)

## What players get
- **Spectator seats.** PLAY ONLINE has a new **JOIN AS SPECTATOR** button next to JOIN. Friends also become spectators automatically when the lobby or the run is full (4 players), when the run is closed to new players (JOIN IN PROGRESS off), during PvP, or right after a run ends. Up to **8 spectators** per host; the 9th is told "That game is full, and so are its 8 spectator seats." A spectator who reconnects (same stable player id) keeps its seat; a player coming back to their own hero is never turned into a spectator (coop2's reclaim comes first).
- **Lobby.** Spectators show under the player cards as **WATCHING** rows (host and everyone else), don't take a player slot, and get no READY UP button. A spectator in the lobby who presses JOIN (team-move key, below) while a slot is free moves into the lobby as a player.
- **Watching a run.** The spectator has no hero. It sees the run as the players do (enemies, bullets, effects, boss bar, banners, timer, score), with its personal HUD hidden (life, gold, gears, Mods, meters, loadout, HUD face, pet, team meter, crosshair, down/buy-back panels).
  - **FOLLOW** any player: CLICK / A next, dash (Space / LB) previous. The bottom card shows the followed player's portrait (expression follows their state), name, life, style rank and weapons; the HUD's life bar and style meter are theirs. If they go down, the camera moves on to someone standing after 2.5 s.
  - **AUTO DIRECTOR** (R / Y cycles FOLLOW > AUTO > FREE CAM): scores every hero (near the boss +3..5, under 35% life up to +3.5, crowds up to +3, style rank x0.6, ultimate +2.5, just hit +0.5) and cuts to the most exciting one: never before 3.5 s on a shot, early if someone is 2.5 better, and after 7 s to the best other hero for variety unless they're far duller. A downed hero is cut away from at once.
  - **FREE CAM**: the move keys / left stick pan (moving switches to it), mouse wheel zooms as usual, clamped 4 m inside the arena edge.
  - **BROADCAST OVERLAY** (Q/E / RB, or O; SETTINGS > BROADCAST OVERLAY sets the default, on): every player's card down the left (portrait, name in their colour, life, style rank, weapons with levels, DOWN / LEFT / ULT! tags, the watched one framed), a blinking LIVE chip, and a clean HUD: objectives, the team list, the style meter and the controls line are hidden for streaming. The camera chips (LIVE · STAGE n · LV n · FOLLOW / AUTO / FREE CAM) stay at the bottom.
  - Menus the players are in show a line: "THE PLAYERS ARE PICKING UPGRADES..." / "...SHOPPING...". When the run ends: a RUN OVER panel with stage and score while waiting for the host (BACK TO LOBBY takes spectators back to the lobby with everyone).
- **Play from the next stage.** T / D-pad down queues a request ("YOU PLAY FROM THE NEXT STAGE"); press again to cancel. When the next stage starts (or while between stages) and the run has a free slot and is joinable, the host hands the spectator to coop2's drop-in path: it waits for a safe moment, then the spectator gets a hero built to the team's level, spawns by a teammate and plays. Its seat is freed.
- **Emotes.** Spectators send coop2's six emotes with 1-6 (pad: D-pad left/right picks, X sends). The host rate-limits them like players' emotes (1 s apart, 4 per 8 s). Everyone in the run sees them in a feed on the left ("MIA WOO-HOO!" with the emote's 8x8 glyph, 4 s, up to 4 lines, a soft UI tick).
- **Players see who's watching**: a "3 WATCHING" badge on the left (online runs only).

## Network (backwards compatible; peer prefix unchanged)
- Client -> host: `hello` gains `spec: 1`; new `spv` {f follow index, free 0/1, x, z} at 4 Hz while it changes (keep-alive every ~1.25 s), `spjoin` {on}, and spectators send coop2's `emote`. Every other message from a spectator peer is swallowed before the player paths (a spectator can't move, fire, pick or ping).
- Host -> spectator: `spwelcome` {why: chosen | full | closed | back}, `spstart` {start, stage, ch, ba, cv, cs} (the run's own `start` and `stage` messages replayed through the normal client path, plus the chests / blown barrels / broken cover catch-up like coop2's `cojoin`), `spplay` (lobby: becoming a player), `roster` when the heroes change. Broadcasts (stage, chests, barrels, cover, route, lobby, lbtolobby) reach spectators as before; `pvpgo`/`pvpstat` are turned into a waiting screen.
- **Snapshots**: spectators get every 2nd snapshot the players get (10 Hz; `spectateTuning.snapEvery`), built by the normal `buildSnapshot` with a `SnapView` (own centre: the followed hero or the free camera point; interest box 1.3 x INTEREST = 65 m; own `SnapEncoder`, keyframe at every run start). Effects drained between two spectator snapshots are buffered so none are lost. Private data is left out: `you.gold`, `you.gears` are 0 and `you.mods` empty (special, passives and the public stats of the followed hero stay, so its build reads right).
- **Event cursors**: skill, time and coop2 send "events since this hero's last snapshot" by a cursor on the hero. Around each spectator build the spectator's own cursors are swapped onto the hero and back (`swapCursors`), so a spectator following a player never eats that player's events. Cursors start at the current sequence on `spstart` (no replay of old events).
- Snapshot field `sp` (generic snapCodec path, absent when nobody watches): `n` count, `w` names, `e` [[seq, name, emote index]] for the last 4 s, and for a spectator `me` [queued, free slots, joinable].
- Lobby broadcast gains `sp` (spectator names). The host's NetHost now accepts 3 + 8 connections (it was 3 + one reconnect spare); the player limits are unchanged and still enforced by the hello paths.
- Bandwidth: a spectator costs about half a player's snapshot stream (10 Hz instead of 20 Hz; a slightly larger box), nothing extra for players except `sp` (0 bytes unless someone watches; ~20 bytes for names while unchanged parts are elided).

## Tuning (`spectateTuning`, src/game/spectate.ts)
max 8 · snapEvery 2 · interestMul 1.3 · viewEvery 0.25 s · freeSpeed 22 m/s (x0.6..1 with zoom) · freeEdge 4 m · cutMin 3.5 s · cutEvery 7 s · cutMargin 2.5 · emoteShow 4 s · feedMax 4. Settings: `spectators` (host rule, on), `spectateOverlay` (on), both in SETTINGS > CO-OP.

## Unfinished / risky
- **PvP spectating** is best effort: spectators wait through the stage phase (the host's solo stage sends no snapshots) and get `spstart` when the duel starts, then mirror it through the co-op snapshot path. pvp2's per-round messages (`p2round`, scoreboard) are broadcast to them too, but the PvP duel with a spectator wasn't run end to end (in-process or in the browser).
- **Spectator input reuse**: spectators use existing actions (dash, reroll, bomb, team, cash/cashpick) and CLICK / A. coop2's ping wheel input still runs on the spectator's machine (its wheel is hidden and the host ignores its pings), and the global autofire key F still toggles the (unused) setting.
- The spectator's local hero index points at the followed hero, so code that reads `game.hero` on a client sees the watched player (that's what drives the HUD). Systems that act on `game.hero` *outside* `clientUpdate` on clients (coop2's buy-back, the time lane's vote key) were checked: their messages are ignored by the host for spectators.
- Not run on real separate machines; the coop script's pages share one machine. Real bandwidth with 8 spectators was not measured (estimate: 8 x half a player stream).
- Help ghosts, the Versus Overlord camera and local co-op aren't touched; a Versus game can be watched like co-op but the Overlord's command view isn't mirrored.
- Nothing here was played by a person.

Spectators send rate-limited emotes that every player sees, and can queue to play from the next stage through coop2's drop-in path when a slot is open. Backwards compatible (new message types, a `spec` hello flag, the `sp` snapshot field; prefix unchanged).
