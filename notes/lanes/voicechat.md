# Lane: voicechat (proximity voice chat)

## What players get
- **VOICE CHAT** (SETTINGS > CO-OP, **off by default**). Turning it on asks for the microphone, and only then. Works in every online mode (co-op lobby and run, Versus, Weekly, PvP lobby and duel) with up to 8 players.
- **Push to talk or open mic** (VOICE MODE): PTT is the default: hold **N** (rebindable: REBIND KEYS > "Push to talk (voice chat)") or **L3** on a pad. While voice chat is on in PTT mode the ultimate uses only R3 (it normally takes L3 or R3); turning voice off or switching to open mic gives L3 back to the ultimate. OPEN MIC sends while you talk louder than **MIC SENSITIVITY** (0..1, default 0.5), with a 0.4 s hang so words don't get clipped. Text fields (lobby chat, names) never trigger PTT.
- **Proximity**: a teammate's voice is full volume within 6 m, fades smoothly to 15% at 25 m ("quiet"), and is silent from 40 m. It pans left/right with where they stand (up to 80%, never fully one-eared). **TEAM RADIO** (off by default) hears everyone at full volume, centred. A downed or spectating player hears everyone at full volume. In the lobby and menus everyone is full volume.
- **Speaking indicators**: a pixel speaker on lobby player cards (top-left corner badge), the co-op teammate list (top-left HUD rows) and the floating name tags: grey = voice on, green with sound waves (stepped blink) = talking, grey with a red cross = muted by you. Players without voice chat show nothing. Your own mic chip sits bottom-left above the loadout during runs and in the lobby: "N TALK" / "MIC" / "TALKING" (green) / "NO MIC" (red).
- **VOICE CHAT screen** (SETTINGS > CO-OP > VOICE PLAYERS & MIC TEST…): the status line (mic ready / waiting for permission / blocked: listen only / no mic), a 20-segment mic meter (-60..0 dB, green while you're sending) with a white line at the open-mic threshold, VOICE CHAT on/off, **TEST MIC (HEAR YOURSELF)** loopback, VOICE MODE, TEAM RADIO, and every player in the game with a speaking light, **volume 0-200% in 25% steps** (- / +) and **MUTE**. Per-player volume and mute are kept by player name in `infinishoot.voicechat.v1` (peer ids change every session). All buttons are `data-nav` (keyboard and pad).
- **Graceful degradation**: no mic or permission denied = listen-only (a silent track is sent so calls still connect) with a toast ("MICROPHONE BLOCKED: YOU CAN STILL LISTEN"); no WebRTC/WebAudio = "VOICE CHAT IS NOT SUPPORTED HERE". Nothing about voice ever blocks the game or the connection.
- **Desktop app**: Electron grants audio capture (never video) via the session permission handlers in electron/main.cjs; on macOS it asks through the system prompt (`systemPreferences.askForMediaAccess('microphone')`), and the Mac build declares `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` plus the `com.apple.security.device.audio-input` entitlement (electron/entitlements.mac.plist, which also carries Electron's usual hardened-runtime JIT entitlements).

## How it works
- **Mesh, no relay**: WebRTC media calls (PeerJS `peer.call`, metadata `{ vc: 1 }`) on the same PeerJS peers the game already uses (`NetHost.voicePeer` / `NetClient.voicePeer`). Every pair of players with voice on has one call; the lower peer id calls (`shouldCall`), the other answers. The host carries nobody else's audio. Mesh with 8 players = 7 Opus streams per machine, ~30 kbit/s each while talking and close to nothing while silent (the sent track is disabled when not talking).
- **Roster over the data channel** (src/game/systems/voicechat.ts): clients send `vchat {on}` on change and every 3 s; the host broadcasts `vroster {r: [peerId, name, on, heroIndex][]}` (lobby order before a run, hero order in it; `heroIndex` -1 in the lobby) on change and every 3 s. **When nobody has voice on, no voice messages are sent at all.** Calls only go to and are only answered from peers in the roster with voice on (unknown callers are refused). Redial backoff 3 s.
- **Audio**: voices play through their own AudioContext (`master volume x VOICE CHAT VOLUME x player volume x proximity`; game ducking, muffling and the health filter never touch them). Each remote stream: muted `<audio>` element (Chromium only feeds remote WebRTC audio to WebAudio while an element plays it) -> MediaStreamSource -> analyser (speaking light: RMS > 0.012, 0.25 s hang) and gain -> StereoPanner -> out. The mic is cloned for the meter/VAD so disabling the sent track doesn't blind the meter. Analysis runs at 30 Hz into one preallocated buffer; UI at 10 Hz. Gains use `setTargetAtTime` (80 ms).
- **Hooks**: `voiceTick(this, dt)` at the top of `Game.update` (runs in menus and the lobby too), `voiceHostMsg` first in `onHostMessage`, `voiceClientMsg` early in `onClientMessage`. Pure functions are exported from src/net/voice.ts for tests.

## Tuning (`voiceTuning`, src/net/voice.ts)
fullDist 6 m, quietDist 25 m, quietGain 0.15, cutDist 40 m, panRange 14 m, panMax 0.8, vadHang 0.4 s, remoteLevel 0.012 RMS, remoteHang 0.25 s, rosterEvery 3 s, smooth 0.08 s, maxPlayerVol 2, redialMs 3000. MIC SENSITIVITY s maps to RMS threshold `0.08 x 10^(-1.5 s)` (0 -> 0.08, 0.5 -> 0.014, 1 -> 0.0025). Settings: `voiceChat` false, `voiceMode` 'ptt', `voiceSensitivity` 0.5, `voiceChatVolume` 0.9, `teamRadio` false.

## Unfinished / risky
- Not tried with real microphones, real speakers or two real machines; echo cancellation relies on the browser's (`echoCancellation: true`): players on speakers with open mic may still leak game audio. PTT is the default for that reason.
- Strict NATs: voice uses the same ICE config (and TURN relay, if configured) as the data channel, so where the game connects, voice should too, but a TURN relay would carry the audio (bandwidth cost).
- 8-player PvP mesh: 7 decode streams per machine; fine on desktop, untested on low-end mobile.
- In the lobby the roster is by lobby index; in a run by hero index. Local co-op players on one machine share that machine's mic (one voice).
- Electron permission handlers allow every non-media permission (the Electron default); the macOS entitlement/usage string only matter once the app is signed with the hardened runtime.
- The ultimate's pad binding changes while PTT voice is on (L3 -> talk, R3 stays ultimate); the CONTROLS legend lists L3 for both with "Push to talk (voice chat)" on its own row.
