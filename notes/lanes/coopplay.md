# Lane: coopplay (co-op that needs teamwork)

Co-op runs get objectives that two heroes have to do together, a way to drag a downed friend out of the fire, a visible buddy link that pays for sticking together, and heroes that talk to each other.

## What players get
- **Co-op objectives.** With 2+ players, each stage has a 60% (seeded) chance to swap its usual objective (totems, beacon, shards, env kills) for one of four co-op ones. They use the same objective panel (top left), the same edge arrow, and the same reward (gold for everyone, a chest per hero, score) plus a TEAMWORK! banner and a 20% heal for everyone standing. At the start of the fight a CO-OP OBJECTIVE banner names it and every piece is pinged (rings, a star and a label: SWITCH A, VAULT, CORE, BEACON, BOMB, BUNKER...). Pieces carry screen markers (pixel tags that slide to the screen edge with an arrow when off-screen) and tall light beams while they still need someone.
  - **TWO-SWITCH VAULT** (`door`): a steel safe with gold trim and two pressure plates 7.5 m either side of it (15 m apart, so one hero can never hold both). Both plates held at once for 1.2 s opens the door ("VAULT OPEN!") and the chests spill out in front. A hero alone on a plate for 3 s pings the other plate ("NEED A BUDDY HERE!") and says "Group up!". HUD: `1/2 ON`, "Need a buddy on the other switch!".
  - **ESCORT THE CORE** (`escort`): a glowing core in a gold ring rolls (1.7 m/s) toward a beacon 26-46 m away while any hero is within 6 m. Enemies treat it as a target (it joins `game.targets`), and every enemy within 1.9 m chews 2.5 life/s off its 100 (up to 5 at once); left alone it heals 1.5/s. Swarmed by 3+ it grinds on at 35% speed. It follows walls around cover (`steerCore` keeps the side it detoured to) and squeezes through if it hasn't got closer for 3 s. Destroyed = objective FAILED ("THE CORE WAS DESTROYED!"). HUD: percent along the road plus the core's life bar.
  - **HOLD TWO ZONES** (`zones`): two rings 16-30 m apart, each ringed by 8 pylons. A hero in each ring at the same time charges 25 s; enemies inside a held ring halve the rate (the ring and pylons flash orange); with only one held it drains 0.5/s.
  - **CARRY THE BOMB** (`bomb`): walk over the bomb to pick it up (65% speed, it rides over your head with a blinking light). Carry it within 2.8 m of the sealed bunker (hazard-striped safe 22-42 m away) or of a boss / mini-boss, and it plants: a red danger zone, 2.5 s fuse, then a blast (5 m, 60 + 25 per stage to enemies, credited as "Team Bomb"). The bunker blows open (collider gone, rubble, chests); a boss loses its shield and 12% of its max life ("SHIELD BLOWN!"). The carrier going down drops it; INTERACT puts it down; you can't carry a teammate and the bomb at once.
  - A hero who leaves mid-objective makes the lone survivor's goal possible: the pieces that need holding at once drop to 1 (`needHeld`).
- **Carry downed teammates.** Walk up to a downed teammate (within 1.9 m) and press **INTERACT** (new rebindable action, H by default; on a gamepad the team button, D-pad down, does it while someone is in reach or you carry something). You lift them onto your shoulders ("CARRYING BUDDY!", `carry_lift` sample) and move at 60%; no revive progress happens while they're carried. Press again to put them down 0.9 m in front ("SAFE! NOW REVIVE!"), then revive as usual. A carrier who falls or drops out puts them down where they stand. The carried player sees "ACE IS CARRYING YOU TO SAFETY"; the carrier sees "[H] PUT BUDDY DOWN"; next to a downed teammate it's "[H] CARRY BUDDY".
- **Buddy tether.** Two living heroes within 7 m are linked (it holds up to 7.8 m so it doesn't flicker). The link is a wavy energy beam between them in their two colours with a white core and two bright beads running along it, faster as it grows. Each linked hero gets +damage and +life regen that grow with time linked: 25% of the full bonus at once, 100% after 20 s: **+3% to +12% damage, +0.25 to +1.0 life/s** (added to live stats after refreshLive, like the team auras). A link that lasted 2 s+ snaps with sparks, a star, "LINK BROKEN" and a crack; link time then drains at 4x. `tether_hum` plays when a link forms (not again for a pair that broke under 2 s ago). HUD panel in the goals stack: "⚡ BUDDY LINK +8% DAMAGE · +0.6 LIFE/S" with a strength bar (MAX at full). SETTINGS > CO-OP > BUDDY TETHER hides the visual (the bonus stays).
- **Co-op banter.** The nine heroes' recorded lines `banter_<hero>_<nicesave|behind|thanks|groupup|onmyway|greatshot>` (unknown heroes borrow the Rookie's), decided on the host and sent on the effects bus as `v:t:<hero>:<line>`, so everyone hears the speaking hero on the shared voice channel (priority 4, between barks and the announcer) with a caption (CAPTIONS):
  - **thanks**: you get up from a teammate's revive (revive progress was past 1 s and a teammate is within 4 m);
  - **nicesave**: a teammate kills an enemy within 3.2 m of you while you're under 40% life (or it was an elite / boss);
  - **behind**: 2+ enemies (or one elite) within 3.6 m behind a teammate's aim; a hero within 16 m of them shouts (checked every 0.5 s);
  - **groupup**: a pinger whose teammates are 14 m+ away on average; also a hero waiting alone on a switch/zone for 3 s;
  - **onmyway**: the nearest teammate answers a ping 1.1 s later if they're 6 m+ away;
  - **greatshot**: the nearest teammate (26 m) when someone kills an elite, mini-boss, boss or Reaper.
  - Cooldowns (`banterTuning`): 5 s between any two lines, 9 s per hero, 22 s before a hero repeats a line. HERO VOICES off = silent and no caption.

## Co-op / protocol (backwards compatible; peer prefix unchanged)
- Client -> host: `carry` (INTERACT pressed). The host picks up the nearest downed teammate in reach or puts down whoever (or the bomb) that hero carries. Old hosts ignore it.
- Host -> clients: snapshot field `cy` (generic snapCodec path): `c` carriedBy per hero, `t` link seconds per hero (whole seconds), `p` linked-pairs bitmask, and while a co-op objective is up `o` = [arena stage, kind index, a.x, a.z, b.x, b.z, v.x, v.z, flags (held A/B, open, core moving), core x/z, bomb x/z, bomb carrier, fuse]. The client installs the objective when the kind first arrives for its arena stage (replacing its seed-rolled solo objective, dropping totem colliders, adding the vault/bunker collider) and drops the bunker collider when it blows. Objective progress/state/life still travels in the arena's `ar.o`.
- Banter rides the effects bus (`v:t:<hero>:<line>`, `v:s:carry_lift`, `v:s:tether_hum`) with no position (heard by everyone). `cy` is small (about 25 numbers while a co-op objective is up; link times go as whole seconds) and the snapshot codec skips it when nothing changed.
- Movement: the carrier's / bomb carrier's slow and a carried client's own position are applied on the machine that moves that hero (`coopPlayLocal` on clients, `slowCarriers` on the host), so client-side movement stays authoritative-feeling.

## Unfinished / risky
- Nothing here was played by people. Balance of the co-op objectives (core life vs late-stage hordes, zone time, bomb blast) is a first guess from the numbers above.
- **Local co-op**: carrying uses this machine's main hero's INTERACT only; other local players' heroes are slowed correctly when they carry the bomb but can't pick up teammates.
- **Gamepad**: the team button doubles as INTERACT only when a downed teammate is in reach or you're carrying; that press then doesn't fire the team move. The keyboard has its own H.
- **The core** follows walls and squeezes through after 3 s stuck; very concave cover clusters could still make it look odd. Enemies chase it like a hero but deal no contact damage to it (it loses life by proximity instead).
- **Boss bombing** needs the boss out of its invulnerable entrance/roar; planting during it still plants, but the blast does nothing to the boss (the objective still completes).
- **Banter** is decided on the host only (clients hear it through the effects bus); a client far from everyone still hears it (no position), which is intended (radio chatter). The voice channel may drop a line if a boss line or the announcer is talking.
- **Old clients** joining a host with this lane ignore `cy`: they keep the solo objective's pieces and HUD (cur/state mirror onto the wrong kind) and don't collide with the vault/bunker.
- Drop-in joiners get `cy` with their first snapshots (installs the objective); pieces' one-off pings at the stage start aren't replayed.

## Decision-log paragraph for PLAN.md
- 2026-10-20: Coopplay lane (notes/lanes/coopplay.md). With 2+ players a stage rolls (60%, seeded) one of four co-op objectives instead of the solo one, on the same objective panel and rewards plus a 20% team heal: a two-switch vault (plates 15 m apart held together 1.2 s), escorting a core (1.7 m/s while a hero is within 6 m; enemies target it and drain 2.5 life/s each), holding two zones at once for 25 s, and carrying a bomb (65% speed) to a sealed bunker or onto a boss (shield gone, 12% of its life). Host-authoritative; clients mirror the snapshot field `cy` and send `carry`.
