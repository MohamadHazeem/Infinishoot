# Lane: world events (the field changes mid-stage)

## What players get
A stage can now have one **world event**: the field itself changes in the middle of the fight. It takes the place of that stage's classic event (meteor shower, gold rush...), so a stage never gets two big ones. The roll comes from the arena seed and the stage number: stage 1 has a 25% chance, later stages 60%. Each biome only offers events that suit it, and the same event never comes twice in a row. It starts 35-60% of the way into the stage, with a banner, and a WORLD EVENT panel in the goals stack (top left) shows what is going on and how long is left.
- **Floor collapse** (not on Sky Fortress). Three waves of three 6 m sections, 6-24 m from the team, crack and glow red for 3.5 s (rumble, dust), then fall into the void. The slab shrinks away into the dark. What's left is a pit: the island's cliff face on its far wall and the backdrop far below (sea, lava, city lights). The holes stay until the stage ends. Enemies on a section fall ("BYE-BYE!"). This counts as an environmental kill; elites and the Reaper only lose 25% of their life. Bosses never fall. Heroes take a hit (12 × stage damage, capped at 35% of their life as always) and are thrown to the nearest solid ground. They never drop in. Sections avoid jump pads and their landings, turrets, totems, shards, the beacon, chests, barrels, pillars and the start plaza.
- **Flash flood** (not on magma or Sky Fortress). After a 3 s warning, water rises over three low areas, the first one under the team. It holds for 30 s and then drains. In the water, heroes move at 70% speed (a dash powers through), enemies are slowed by up to 40%, Burn is put out (with a puff of steam), and a shocked enemy arcs Shock to wet enemies within 5 m (up to 16 a tick, every 0.4 s).
- **Island split** (not on Sky Fortress). A crack glows for 4 s with rumbles. Then a 4 m chasm opens right across the island, just up-screen of the team, and stays. Three 5.5 m plank bridges with rope rails cross it. Bullets fly over it. Anything on the line when it opens is dropped or thrown clear, as in a collapse.
- **The Colossus** (every biome). A stone giant crosses the field 5-9 m beside the team in 14 strides, one every 1.6 s. A huge body shadow sweeps ahead of it. Its feet come down from above the screen onto red footprint telegraphs that appear 1.4 s ahead, with an off-screen BOSS sound cue. A stomp (3.6 m) hits heroes (30 × stage damage, capped), flattens non-boss enemies ("SQUASH!", an environmental kill), throws everything within 7.5 m outward, sets off barrels and leaves a crater. Every step rumbles the whole field a little. Steps off the island splash into the sea.
- **Eclipse** (day stages only). The sun goes out over 5 s and stays dark for 25 s, using the night look: hero lanterns and glowing enemy eyes. Enemies move 15% faster in the dark, new spawns included. Then the sun bursts back with a flash, and every enemy within 30 m of a hero is dazzled (60% slow for 2.5 s). With REDUCE FLASHING on, the flash is the HUD's reduced flash and the light fades back instead of snapping.
- **Giant flower** (not on magma or Neon City). A bud sprouts on walkable ground 8-13 m from the team, and an edge arrow points to it. It grows for 6 s, then blooms into a 4.5 m healing ring for 22 s: heroes in it heal 4% of their max life per second, and pollen slows enemies in it by 30%. Then it wilts.
- **Eruption** (meadow, dunes, peaks, magma; twice as likely on magma). A volcano rumbles at the shore on the team's side for 3 s. Then for 25 s, lava bombs (one every 0.65 s) arc in from its direction onto red circles near random heroes. Each bomb gives 1.5 s of warning and a mortar whistle on the threat bus. A bomb hits heroes for 16 × stage damage and enemies for 40 × the horde's HP scaling (an environmental kill), and ignites enemies and barrels. It leaves a glowing lava puddle for 6 s that burns heroes (5 × stage damage per 0.6 s, i-frames apply) and ignites enemies.
- **Night Raid: a stealth stage.** It shows up as a new route-map node, Night Raid. It also replaces the roll on 35% of night stages (Neon City counts as night). The stage is dark, with the hero lantern dimmed. 14 lamp posts throw pools of light, and two searchlight towers sweep beams. Enemies start unaware and stroll around where they spawned. Each one notices a hero who is close and visible: 3.2 m in the dark, up to 13 m fully lit, and elites see 40% farther. Firing your left click lights you up and makes noise: 9 m, or 13 m for the Scattergun and Rail Driver. A dash makes noise to 4.5 m, and a special or the bomb to 22 m. Getting hit always gives you away. The right click is quiet. A noticed enemy turns **suspicious** (a yellow "?") and walks to where it saw you; if it keeps seeing you it becomes **alert** (a red "!", a "!" pop and a shout). An alert enemy hunts you and alerts every enemy within 7 m, wave by wave. It calms down after 9 s without seeing you. Unaware enemies show a closed eye. Auto weapons, pets and turrets hold fire at unaware enemies, so a careful team really can sneak. Three **intel caches** (crates under cyan light columns, each next to a lamp, with an edge arrow to the nearest) take 1.5 s of standing on. Each pays (6 + 2 × stage) gold to everyone and 600 × stage score, doubled (GHOST BONUS) when no hunter is within 14 m. Taking all three drops a treasure chest per hero. The HUD panel shows a light meter (eye icon and 8 segments, HIDDEN / LIT, a LOUD chip while you make noise), the intel count, how many enemies are hunting you, and a grab bar. When the boss lands, the raid ends: everything hunts. The dev bot simply goes loud.

## Co-op and determinism
- The host rolls the event and runs every timeline and all damage. The snapshot carries two fields:
  - `we` = { k: kind index, p: the plan numbers, d: done }. It only changes when an event starts or ends, so the compact codec leaves it out of almost every snapshot.
  - `wt` = { t: event clock } plus the lava bombs and puddles (`b`, `q`) and the raid's caches, hunter count, grab progress and awareness (`g`, `a`, `gp`, `aw` = host uid + level for the suspicious/alert enemies within 26 m of that client, at most 80).

  Both go through the snapshot codec's generic fallback, so there is no codec change.
- Clients rebuild the same event from (k, p) with the same pure functions (src/game/worldEvents.ts) and add or remove the same gap colliders from the event clock. Each change rebuilds the walls grid, as breakable cover and barrels do. The client's clock runs on locally between snapshots and snaps back when it is more than 0.25 s off.
- Each machine moves its own hero: a client slows its hero in flood water and steps it out of a hole by itself (`worldEventLocal`), like jump pads and quicksand. Damage and knockback come from the host as usual.
- Plans use the arena seed, the stage, the event kind and the team's centre when the event starts. Seeds are mulberry32 (`Rng`). Stealth patrol spots are hashed from the enemy uid.

## Test links (src/dev/worldEventLinks.ts)
- `&wev=<kind>` (collapse, flood, split, giant, eclipse, flower, volcano, stealth) forces this stage's event, 1 s in (`&wevat=<s>`). `&wevnow` starts it at once. `&wevt=<s>` jumps its clock once it runs. `&wevoff` turns world events off.
- Examples: `?test&god&wev=collapse&wevt=12.5&zoom=1.7` (holes plus the next wave cracking), `?test&god&wev=split&wevt=4&zoom=1.5`, `?test&god&wev=flood&wevt=10`, `?test&god&wev=giant&wevt=9&zoom=1.5`, `?test&god&wev=eclipse&wevt=8`, `?test&god&wev=flower&wevt=6`, `?test&god&stage=4&wev=volcano&wevt=6`, `?test&god&wev=stealth&spawn=swarmer:6,bopper:3&spawnr=7`, `?test&stage=2&node=stealth` (the route node).

## Files
- New: `src/game/worldEvents.ts` (pure: data, tuning, roll, plans, geometry, timelines, light; `worldHooks`), `src/game/systems/worldEvents.ts` (Game methods: flow, timelines, colliders, net, hero rescue, render hook), `src/game/systems/stealth.ts` (awareness, patrol lure, caches, stealth net), `src/render/worldEventView.ts` (everything drawn), `src/ui/worldEventHud.ts` (panel and edge arrow, CSS injected), `src/ui/debugWorldEvents.ts`, `src/dev/worldEventLinks.ts`, `tests/worldEvents.test.ts`, `tests/worldEventsGame.test.ts`.
- Shared files, small additive hooks:
  - `game.ts`: import, six method installs, `this.updateWorldEvent(dt)` after `updateWind` in `simulate`.
  - `systems/render.ts`: `this.renderWorldEvent(rdt)` after `renderArena`.
  - `systems/netHost.ts`: `we` and `wt` in the snapshot body. `systems/netClient.ts`: `applyWorldEventNet(s.we, s.wt)` and `worldEventLocal(dt)` (next to `arenaLocal`).
  - `enemies.ts`: optional `EnemyCtx.lure(e)`. When it returns a spot, the enemy walks there at `speed` × its own and doesn't attack (`targetAlive` false). Six lines in the target pick.
  - `systems/combat.ts`: `nearestEnemy` skips `worldHooks.hidden(e)` (unaware enemies on a raid).
  - `systems/look.ts`: `lookOverrides.eventNight` (extra darkness, max'ed with the stage's), `nightSnap`, `lantern` (scale), and `lookHooks` (extra lights each frame).
  - `runEvents.ts`: `suppressEvent()`.
  - `data/routeNodes.ts`: the `stealth` node kind (Night Raid, weight 2, glyph ◐ until it has icon art).
  - `dev/testLinks.ts`, `ui/debugPanel.ts`: one call each.

## For other lanes
- `worldEventOf(game)` gives the running event (kind, t, p, done, boxes, bombs, puddles, stealth). `startWorldEvent(game, kind)` starts one on the host (cinematics or modes can use it). `worldEventForce.kind` forces one per stage.
- `lookOverrides.eventNight` and `lookHooks` are generic. Anything can darken the stage or add lights through them.
- `EnemyCtx.lure` is generic. Any mode (pacifist route, rhythm stage) can make enemies walk somewhere instead of chasing.
- Sound lane: the events use existing cues (`ominous`, `jingle`, `coverBreak`, `bossSlam`, `explode`, `powerUp`, `heal`, `chest`, `telegraph`, plus `threatMortar` and `bossWindup` on the threat bus). Dedicated sounds (colossus footsteps, flood rush, eclipse hum, alert stinger, a COLOSSUS / LAVA threat label) would be a nice follow-up.

## Verification
- `npm run typecheck`, `npm test` (40 files, including the 2 new ones) and `npm run smoke` pass. The smoke's standard bot took a Night Raid route node and played through it.
- `tests/worldEvents.test.ts` (pure):
  - The roll is deterministic and stays in its window, with rates near the tuned 25% / 60%. Night Raids happen only at night (35%). The biome filters hold, there is no eclipse at night, and a kind doesn't repeat on the next stage.
  - Collapse plans stay 6-24 m from the team and off the plaza, respect `avoid`, and come out the same every time. Split bridges are never blocked and the chasm covers most of the run.
  - The flood, eclipse and flower timelines run in order. The colossus keeps a steady stride, alternates feet and passes beside the team.
  - Stealth layouts are deterministic and avoid blocked spots. A spot under a lamp is lit and a spot between lamps is dark. Sight grows with light.
- `tests/worldEventsGame.test.ts` (real Game, jsdom):
  - Same seed, same plan for stages 1, 2, 3 and 5.
  - Solo collapse: the sections become gap colliders and the walls grid is rebuilt; walls block walking there but bullets pass; the enemy on a section falls as an environmental kill; the hero is hit and thrown clear (this found a 0.2 m seam between neighbouring sections that trapped heroes, fixed with seamless cells and a rescue step); the holes stay, and the next stage has whole ground.
  - Eclipse speed-up, burst, restore and dazzle work. The flower heals.
  - Host and client in-process: the same chasm plan and gap boxes on both, walls blocked and bridge open on both, all gone after the next stage. The client wades at 70%. Lava bombs reach the client.
  - Night Raid: an enemy 8 m away in the dark stays unaware and patrols, and `nearestEnemy` skips it. A hit alerts it and the enemy next to it. Hunter counts match on host and client, the client marks the hunting enemies, a cache pays and the take mirrors, and the boss ends the raid.
- Cost:
  - In jsdom, with 250 enemies, `renderWorldEvent` takes 0.06 ms a frame with no event, 0.07-0.09 ms with a flood, colossus or collapse, and 0.19 ms on a Night Raid (markers over every nearby enemy).
  - `npm run bench -- --dev` on the heavy scene (headless SwiftShader, about 1.5 fps, so only 30 frames are measured and the numbers are noisy): `sim.hazards+arena` was 0.19 ms per step with events off, 0.29 with a collapse and 0.40 with a Night Raid (awareness for about 280 enemies). Draw calls went from 136 to 134-137. `ren.barrels+arena+chests` (which now includes the event's look) read 0.4 ms off and about 2 ms with an event. I believe that is one-time building (textures, merged meshes) landing in the few measured frames, since the per-frame jsdom figure above is small, but I didn't confirm it on a real GPU.
- Screenshots looked at (SwiftShader): collapse (cracks with the red danger fill, pits with cliff faces), split with a bridge, flood, the colossus foot and leg with SQUASH! and ENVIRONMENTAL KILL!, the eclipse's darkness with the lantern, the flower in bloom, the eruption on magma, the Night Raid with lamps, ? and closed-eye markers, the HUD meter and cache arrow.

## Unfinished / risky
- **No colossus on the horizon.** The fixed 3/4 camera never sees past the field edge (the sky lane's note): anything beyond about 11 m up-screen of the hero is under the view, however tall. So the giant crosses the field beside the team instead of walking behind it. You see its feet stamping in from above the screen, its legs rising out of view and its shadow. If a later cinematic camera looks at the horizon, a full-body silhouette would be a small addition to `WorldEventView.buildGiant`.
- **Holes are painted, not cut.** The ground and the island base are single meshes, so a pit is flat quads drawn like a pit from the fixed camera (cliff face on the far wall, dark backdrop below, dark lips). Debris and gems lying in a hole still sit on top of it. The magnet pulls gems out.
- **Enemies don't path around holes or the chasm.** They steer straight and slide along walls (no flow field yet), so hordes pile up at a chasm edge and along hole rims. That makes split stages easier: shooting across the chasm is strong. If it feels too safe, lower `bridgeSpread` or add bridges.
- **Night Raid balance hasn't been played.** Patrols plus auto weapons that hold fire mean a sneaking player kills less and levels slower. That is the trade, but the cache rewards are first guesses. Ring spawns keep coming in, and they are unaware too. The director's horde size isn't reduced.
- **Client timing.** A client's event clock follows the 20 Hz snapshots, so a hole can open up to a frame or two away from the host's moment. The client's own collision follows its own clock. During the boss the timer is hidden but the event clock still comes in `we.t`.
- **Overlapping flood zones** draw one foam edge across the other's water.
- **Shock spread** credits the nearest hero, not the hero who shocked the first enemy. No lane tracks a shock's owner.
- **Not verified:** real GPUs, `npm run coop` (it needs the PeerJS broker), touch, the Electron build. No balance runs with the bot beyond smoke.
