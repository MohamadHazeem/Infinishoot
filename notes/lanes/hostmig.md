# Lane: hostmig (host migration)

## What players get
- **The run survives the host.** If the host quits, closes the tab, crashes or loses its connection mid-run, the run goes on with a new host instead of ending for everyone. Everyone sees **NEW HOST: BRAVO** (the `host_migrate` sample plus the announcer's "New host. Keep fighting!", captioned), and clients see a small **HOST LEFT · NEW HOST · BRAVO · CONNECTING...** panel while they move over (**HOST LOST · FINDING A NEW HOST...** while the old host is still being probed).
- **Nothing is lost.** Every hero keeps its whole build (loadout ranks and evolutions, auto weapons, passives, Mods and their levels, relics, special), gold, gears, banishes, life, revives, ultimate and style; the team keeps its level, XP, score, kills, combo, stage clock, director state (spawn budget, elites, rings, Reaper timer, Goldbug), curses, run event and challenge, route map, chests, every enemy (life, elite modifier, shield, boss phase, mutation) and every pickup on the ground.
- **Menus come back.** A host lost during a level-up, the shop (offers, locks, rerolls, the secret merchant's relics and the curse deal included), or the route vote reopens it on the new host; returning players get theirs again the moment they are back. For `hostmigTuning.hold` (30 s) menus wait for migrating players instead of the autopilot answering for them.
- **The old host's hero** goes on the coop2 autopilot for the usual 60 s grace and then leaves by the drop-out rules (its build is kept in the stash, as for any leaver).
- **Alone?** If only one player is left, they carry on solo (**YOU CARRY ON**), with the shop or level-up still open if one was.
- **Two in a row** work: the new host shares migration snapshots of its own (epoch 1, 2, ...), so it can be replaced too.

## How it works
- `src/net/migration.ts` (pure, unit tested): the migration snapshot's binary enemy and pickup records (~25 B per enemy, 9 B per pickup, every live one rather than a client's interest box), `prims`/`applyPrims` (a lane object's own primitive fields: director, adaptive pace, run events, boss field, RNG, style meter, gameplay/pet state), `electHost`, `baseCode`, `hostmigTuning`.
  - PeerJS's binarypack throws on Infinity and integers it can't fit (found by `npm run migrate`: the director's `goldenAt = Infinity` broke the host loop). `prims` sends those as `#num:<value>` text and `applyPrims` turns them back into numbers; every `mig` send is also wrapped so a packing error can never stop the host.
- `src/game/systems/hostmig.ts` (the glue):
  - **Host**: `mig` message to every connected client every 1.5 s, and at once when the mode, stage, route phase, connected roster or any build changes (a cheap build fingerprint every 10 steps). Heroes travel as `exportBuild` HeroBuilds (the PvP/coop2 stash path) plus gold, life, position, pending picks, offers and shop state. `cands` lists the hero indexes the host still has connected (not on the autopilot). Leaving on purpose (`leaveToTitle`) or a closing tab (`pagehide`) sends a fresh snapshot plus `hmig`; clients then drop the old connection and migrate at once.
  - **Client**: keeps the last snapshot. When the connection is lost the netcode lane's reconnect loop starts; unless the old host answers within `probe` (4 s; 0 after `hmig`), every client elects the same new host from the same snapshot: the lowest join order (hero index) in `cands`, skipping anyone it couldn't reach. The elected player promotes itself; the others repoint the reconnect loop (`rc.code`) at the new room `<code>-M<epoch>H<index>` (`migrationCode`, src/net/peer.ts) and keep their 60 s reconnect window. A candidate silent for `candidate` (16 s) is skipped for the next. If the old host answered but never lets the client back in within `blip` (15 s), it probes again.
  - **Healing a split**: a new host (or a player who went solo) that skipped an earlier candidate it couldn't reach keeps trying that candidate's room for `heal` (90 s) while nobody has joined it; if it answers, it closes its own room and joins as a client (its hero waits on that host's autopilot and is reclaimed).
  - **Promotion** (`promote` / `restoreRun`): role -> host (or solo), a `NetHost` on the derived room, the simulation rebuilt: enemies, pickups and chests replaced by the snapshot's, heroes rebuilt with `applyBuild` and their numbers, the mirror kept where it is newer (positions, life, own gold, score/kills, a level gained after the snapshot). Every other hero joins the coop2 grace list (autopilot), coop2's player ids come from the snapshot, so a returning `hello` reclaims the right hero through the normal drop-in path (`start` + `stage` + `cojoin`); coop2 lets migrating players in mid-menu (`hostmigAdmitNow`) and holds their menu answers (`hostmigHolds`); when they are back the host re-presents their level-up/shop/wait screen and the route.
  - Edge cases: level-up (resumes and reopens for whoever still has picks), shop (exact offers and gold), route vote (reopened), boss intro (skipped; the boss is just there), finisher (a boss at 0 life dies on the new host at once), stage transition (loads the next stage if the old host hadn't yet), lag (the netsim client in `npm run migrate`), two migrations, solo fallback.

## Tuning (`hostmigTuning`, src/net/migration.ts)
- `every` 1.5 s between snapshots (plus at once on a change); `recent` 8 s (a player whose connection just dropped is still a candidate: a dying machine's connections often go a moment before it does, and every client must elect from the same list); `probe` 4 s; `blip` 15 s; `candidate` 16 s, x`aliveMul` 3 while the room is found but not yet reachable; `heal` 90 s; `hold` 30 s. The last snapshot is used however old it is (a starved machine can take a minute to notice the loss).
- Size: a 4-player fight with ~50 enemies is ~8 KB per snapshot (binarypack), mostly hero builds and level-up offers; 300 enemies add ~7.5 KB. At one per 1.5 s that is ~5-10 KB/s per client next to ~44 KB/s of regular snapshots.
- CPU: host only, with clients connected: a build fingerprint every 10 steps and a snapshot every 1.5 s. Solo play adds one state lookup per step, so `npm run bench` (solo) is unaffected (not run: the machine was at load 60-110).

## Protocol (additive; same peer prefix)
- Host -> client: `mig` (the migration snapshot, `v` 1), `hmig` {bye} (leaving now).
- New rooms: `infinishoot-v4-<CODE>-M<epoch>H<heroIndex>`; `roomCode()` keeps that suffix where plain codes are normalized to 5 characters.

## Unfinished / risky
- **Real internet not tested**: headless pages on one machine through the public PeerJS broker. ICE on real networks (TURN, strict NATs) can make a candidate unreachable for one player but not another; the split healing covers the case where the one who went it alone can later reach the others' host, not two groups that can never reach each other.
- **A host that is alive but cut off from everyone** keeps playing alone with every hero on the autopilot (as before), while the others migrate. Nothing merges the two runs.
- **Host-only lane state is rebuilt fresh**: boss move queues and arena twists, the Titan's parts, world events and stealth stages in progress, mini-games, time-lane votes and rewind charges, MVP tallies, the gameplay lane's crates and mech suits on the ground, Versus and PvP (not migrated at all: a PvP or Versus host leaving still ends the match). A migration during those plays on with defaults.
- A boss intro is skipped by a migration; a finisher in progress ends with the boss dying at once.
- The old host's player can't rejoin by reloading: hosts don't keep the tab-reload record (their hero leaves after the grace).
- Up to 1.5 s of progress can roll back (XP, enemy positions), except what the clients' mirrors already had newer (positions, life, own gold, score, kills, level).
- Snapshot size grows with level-up offers and hero count; a 4-player late-loop fight could reach ~20 KB per snapshot (PeerJS chunks it).
