# Lane: MP (Versus Swarm, local co-op, lobby browser, weekly co-op board)

## What players get
- **Versus Swarm** (PLAY ONLINE > host > GAME: VERSUS, or PLAY ONLINE > VERSUS VS AI to practise). One player is the **Overlord**, picked in the lobby (the host, any guest, or the AI); everyone else plays heroes. The match is one stage (4:00). The normal director spawns nothing: the Overlord's command view (camera high above the field, pan with WASD / arrows / left stick) shows a points meter and a card bar of ten cards:

  | Card | Cost | Cooldown | Unlocks | What |
  |---|---:|---:|---:|---|
  | Swarm Pack | 6 | 1.5 s | 0:00 | 10 Swarmers |
  | Bopper Gang | 10 | 2.5 s | 0:00 | 6 Boppers |
  | Zippy Rush | 12 | 4 s | 0:20 | 6 Zippies |
  | Meteor Strike | 18 | 10 s | 0:30 | 5 telegraphed meteors where you click |
  | Blaster Squad | 16 | 6 s | 0:40 | 3 Blaster Bots |
  | Elite | 40 | 20 s | 0:45 | one elite (random type, modifier, escorts) |
  | Bullet Nova | 14 | 7 s | 0:50 | a 1 s telegraph, then 18 enemy bullets in a ring |
  | Rhino Stampede | 20 | 8 s | 1:00 | 3 Rhinos |
  | Chonk Wall | 22 | 8 s | 1:15 | 2 Chonks |
  | Unleash the Boss | 100 | once | 2:30 | the stage boss; at 4:00 it comes anyway |

  Number keys 1-9/0, Q / E or LB / RB pick a card; click (or A / Space at the screen centre) drops it. Enemy groups land at one of six green **beacons** if you click near one (closed while a hero stands within 10 m) or else at the **field edge** in the clicked direction (slid along the edge, never within 10 m of a hero); hazards land where you click. The cursor says where the drop would land or why it can't ("COOLDOWN", "BEACON BLOCKED BY A HERO"...), checked with the same rules the host uses. Heroes see an OVERLORD panel with its purse, and a toast for each order ("THE OVERLORD SENDS RHINO STAMPEDE!").
  **Win:** heroes survive the timer and beat the boss (they still level up, shop at the start, find chests); the Overlord wins when every hero is down. A result banner plays on every screen, then everyone goes back to the title (nothing is banked, like PvP). If the Overlord's player leaves, the AI Overlord takes over its purse.
- **Local co-op** (title > LOCAL CO-OP): 2-4 players on one PC. Each gamepad presses A to join (the keyboard presses ENTER and always takes player 1), picks its hero and loadout with up/down (row) and left/right (value), and presses A when ready; B leaves, Y / M switches STANDARD / WEEKLY CO-OP. The run starts 2 s after everyone is ready. In the run every hero reads its own pad (player 1 the keyboard and mouse, or its pad), the camera frames the whole team and zooms out with the spread (up to x2), and a 15 m leash holds anyone who runs off. Each player has a panel on the right (life, special meter, device) and pad players an aim marker. Level-up cards and the shop go round in turn ("P2 · PAD 1: YOUR PICK"), with the shared menus listening to that player's pad. Any pad's Start pauses, and that pad drives the pause menu. Co-op rules are the online ones: revives, co-op items, enemy scaling, the TEAM meter.
- **Lobby browser**: hosts switch **PUBLIC** on in their lobby; the game registers it on the online service (name, code, players, game type, difficulty, region from the time zone, version) with a heartbeat. PLAY ONLINE > BROWSE GAMES lists open lobbies with REGION / DIFFICULTY / GAME filters (region defaults to yours), refreshes every 15 s, greys out other versions, and joins on click. Without the service the screen says how to enable it (VITE_LEADERBOARD_URL) and invite codes work as before.
- **Weekly co-op challenge**: GAME: WEEKLY in an online lobby (or WEEKLY CO-OP mode in local co-op) plays this ISO week's Weekly Challenge (src/data/weekly.ts: seed, two rules, Rookie kit for everyone) as a team. PLAY ONLINE > WEEKLY CO-OP shows the week's rules, the TEAM NAME the host plays under, this PC's best team result, and the week's global TEAM board (one entry per team per week; a team is its name plus its members).

## Knobs
- `versusTuning` (src/game/versus.ts): points 30 at start, 3.2/s rising to 6.4/s by the end, cap 200; rate limit 0.4 s between orders and at most 6 per 5 s; beacon click radius 6 m, beacon block 10 m, edge-to-hero 10 m; max 280 alive for groups; meteor 18 / nova 10 damage x the stage's enemy damage scaling; AI thinks every 1.1 s, `aiSave` 0.35; command view zoom 2.2; result banner 5 s. Cards in `OVERLORD_CARDS`.
- `localCoopTuning` (src/game/localCoop.ts): leash 15 m, zoom from 9 m spread at 0.055/m up to x2, start countdown 2 s.
- `lobbyTuning` (src/net/lobbies.ts): heartbeat 45 s, re-send at most every 10 s on changes. Worker: `LOBBY_TTL` 120 s, heartbeats faster than 8 s refused, 6 new lobbies per IP per 10 minutes (server/leaderboard/lobbies.js).
- Settings: `teamName` (WEEKLY CO-OP screen), `lobbyPublic` (the lobby's PUBLIC toggle).
- Debug panel folder "Multiplayer: versus, local co-op" (all three tuning objects, Versus vs AI, Fill Overlord points).

## How it works
- **Versus** (src/game/versus.ts pure rules; src/game/systems/versus.ts Game methods; src/ui/versusUi.ts HUD). `game.versus` (`VersusRun`) holds the purse (`OverlordState`), beacons, pending novas and the result. Hooks: `Game.simulate` passes the director's requests through `versusReqs` (only the 4:00 boss survives, and only if the Overlord hasn't sent it) and calls `versusStep` (purse, AI, novas, win check); the Overlord's own hero is benched (`left` + dead, weapons disposed) so it is never simulated, targeted or revived, and `hostTick` still sends it snapshots; its `vcam` messages move that bench hero so the snapshot interest box follows its camera. Orders: `vcmd {c, x, z}` (client) or a direct call (host / AI), validated by `validateCommand` (card, unlock time, cooldown, points, boss once, alive cap, rate limit, placement); refusals go back as `vsres`. Snapshot field `vs`: `[overlord, points, bossUsed, winner, cooldown tenths x10]` (~25 bytes, generic snapCodec field). Result: `vsend {w, name}`. The win check turns the mode to 'dead' with `gameOverShown` set, so `endRun` never runs (no game over, no banking). Stage events are suppressed (`runEvents.suppressEvent()`); world events still run.
- **Local co-op** (src/game/localCoop.ts join model and helpers; src/game/systems/localCoop.ts; src/core/padInput.ts; src/ui/localCoopUi.ts). `game.localCoop` (`LocalCoopRun`) holds the slots and a `PadInput` per pad hero. `Game.updateHero` starts with `localHeroUpdate`: a pad hero's update runs with `game.input` swapped for its PadInput and `game.local` set to it, so every "local hero" path (aim, dash, fire, charge, special, parry, ultimate, rumble) uses that pad without per-system changes; then the leash. `Input.padIndex` / `usePad()` pick which gamepad the shared Input reads (-1 first connected as before, -2 none, n that pad), so player 2's stick can't move player 1. `localCoopTick` (every update) hands level-up / shop turns round and gives the shared Input to the turn player's pad. `role` stays 'solo'.
- **Lobby browser** (src/net/lobbies.ts client, server/leaderboard/lobbies.js, src/ui/lobbyBrowser.ts; heartbeat in src/game/systems/mp.ts): lobby metadata rides on KV key metadata so one `list()` serves the screen; each host has a random token (made on first listing). The heartbeat unlists when the game starts or the host leaves.
- **Team board** (src/net/teamboard.ts client and local best `teamweekly.v1`, server/leaderboard/teamboard.js, src/ui/teamBoard.ts): `mpRunBanked` (hook in `bankAndShow`) records the team's best on every machine of a weekly run with 2+ heroes and the host / local PC posts it. Online weekly co-op works by `startRun` keeping `runMode` 'weekly' when the lobby kind is weekly; clients already got the week through the start message's `meta`.
- Lobby: the GAME row has CO-OP / PVP / VERSUS / WEEKLY (`LobbyKind` in screens.ts); `screens.onLobby` lets src/game/systems/mp.ts draw the Overlord pick, the weekly team and PUBLIC; the host's `lobby` message carries them as `mp` (`mpLobbyExtra`).

## Unfinished / risky
- Not tested with real hardware or networks: no real gamepads (fake pads in tests and links; the Gamepad API index mapping is standard but untested on devices), no real two-machine Versus (`npm run coop` can't reach the PeerJS broker here), the worker routes ran only against a fake KV (not deployed). Nothing was play-tested by a person; the Versus economy and card costs are first guesses tuned on the bot (it doesn't dodge and plays in god mode).
- Cloudflare free KV allows 1,000 writes a day: public lobby heartbeats (80/hour per lobby) cover about 12 hours of public hosting a day in total (server/leaderboard/README.md). KV `list()` is eventually consistent, so new lobbies can take up to a minute to appear.
- Local co-op: only player 1 has keyboard-only extras (team move T, combo cash-in X, rewind B and pings read the shared Input for player 1; pad players get them through pad buttons where those systems read pad buttons). CHARGE MODE "toggle" shares one latch (src/game/chargeMode.ts) between local heroes; hold mode (default) is fine. Menus during a pad player's turn also accept the keyboard. Hero 0's loadout pick is written to `settings.loadout` (in memory), so player 1's local pick becomes their solo default if settings are saved later.
- Versus: elites can fall back to the normal ring spawn near a hero if their jittered spot is in a wall (spawnEnemy); the boss enters through `spawnBoss` near the heroes (not at the clicked spot). World events (wave 1) still run in Versus. A client Overlord's input travels only as orders and camera focus; its own 'i' messages are ignored by the host. Mid-run joining (COOP lane) wasn't considered for Versus: a hero joining mid-match would be a normal hero.
- Shared-file merge points (small hooks): game.ts (imports, method installs, `versus` / `localCoop` fields, `LobbyKind`, `installMp` in the constructor, `mpTick` after `coopInput`, `versusReqs` around `director.update`, `versusStep` after the duel step, `localHeroUpdate` at the top of `updateHero`), screens.ts (`ExtraScreen` ids, `LobbyKind`, `LobbyView.kind/extra`, `onLobby`, the GAME row's two new types and notes, HOST HEAT only for co-op), online.ts (lobby extras in `refreshLobby`, Versus/Weekly in `hostStart`), netHost.ts (`versusHostMessage`, snapshots to a benched Overlord, `vs` field), netClient.ts (`mpClientMessage`, lobby kinds and extras, `applyVersusNet`), runFlow.ts (weekly `runMode` for online weekly, `mpReset` in `leaveToTitle`, `mpRunBanked` in `bankAndShow`), render.ts (`mpFrame` after `bossCamera`), input.ts (`padIndex`, `usePad`, exported `applyDeadzone`), settings.ts (`teamName`, `lobbyPublic`), testLinks.ts, debugPanel.ts, smoke.test.ts, server/leaderboard/routes.js. If the COOP lane changes `hello` handling (drop-in), the lobby `mp` extras and Versus bench rules need a look.

## Decision-log paragraph for PLAN.md
- 2026-10-19: MP lane (notes/lanes/mp.md). Versus Swarm: an online game type (GAME: VERSUS; also VERSUS VS AI for practice) where one player, picked in the lobby (host, guest or the AI), is the Overlord. It watches from high above and spends points (30, then 3.2/s rising to 6.4/s, cap 200) on ten cards with cooldowns and unlock times (groups of Swarmers to Chonks, an elite, meteor and bullet-nova hazards, the boss at 2:30 for 100); groups drop at six beacons (closed near heroes) or at the field edge, hazards where clicked. The director's spawns are replaced (only the 4:00 boss gets through if the Overlord never sent it); the host validates and rate-limits every order (0.4 s apart, 6 per 5 s) with the same rules the Overlord's cursor previews. Heroes win by surviving and beating the boss, the Overlord by downing everyone; nothing is banked. The Overlord's hero is benched (`left` and dead) but still gets snapshots, its camera focus steering its interest box. Local co-op: 2-4 players on one PC (keyboard player 1 plus pads), a join screen, one framing camera with a 15 m leash, per-player panels, and level-up/shop menus that go round in turn; pad heroes run the normal hero update with their own PadInput swapped in, so every system works unchanged, and the shared Input can now be told which pad to read. Lobby browser: hosts can list a lobby as PUBLIC on the optional worker (heartbeat 45 s, TTL 120 s, host token, metadata on KV keys); BROWSE GAMES filters by region (from the time zone), difficulty and game type. Weekly co-op: GAME: WEEKLY (or local WEEKLY CO-OP) plays the Weekly Challenge as a team, with a per-week TEAM board on the worker (best run per team name plus members) and a local best.
