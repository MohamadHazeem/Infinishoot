# Biomes lane: Neon City and Sky Fortress

## What players get
- **Neon City** (night rooftops): wet violet roof and slick street tiles, cover walls with neon tubes, pixel-glyph neon signs (on walls, on poles, and a row along the far parapet), neon street lamps, glowing vending machines and AC units, 5 flickering hologram billboards (they glitch now and then), steam vents that puff in cycles, puddles with neon streaks, a glowing helipad on the start plaza, and a skyline of lit towers around the roof, with the city lights far below. Signs, lamps and holograms are real dynamic lights on the ground, and each one leaves a streaky reflection on the wet roof (additive ground quads, no extra render pass). Enemies get a violet/neon palette and cyan glowing eyes. It rains on 60% of Neon City stages; the rain only darkens the night a little (no grey day-rain sky). Dry nights get drifting neon dust motes.
- **Sky Fortress** (floating islands): pale flagstone islands with plank boardwalks, split by two 4 m chasms (one along each axis) plus a few small pits. Every chasm half has a 6 m plank bridge with rope rails, so every tile stays reachable from the start. Chasms block walking but bullets fly over them. Cover walls get battlements; there are battlement edges, up to 12 flags (some on pillars) that stream downwind, three toy airships drifting along the far side and a flank, floating islets, a drifting cloud-puff layer and a cloud sea far below.
- **Wind** (Sky Fortress): a seeded breeze plus one gust about every 11 s. It bends every bullet (enemy shots fully, yours at 55%) and blows light enemies (xp ≤ 2, not elites or bosses) around. White streaks show its direction, flags stream with it, steam drifts with it, and each gust plays `wind_gust`. An enemy knocked hard (knockback above `hazardTuning.knockSpeed`) toward a chasm **falls**: "BYE-BYE!", counted as an environmental kill ('Sky Falls' in the damage breakdown; elites and the Reaper only lose 25% of max life).
- **Rotation**: loop 1 (stages 1-4) is unchanged. From stage 5 the areas cycle `neon, dunes, sky, magma, meadow, peaks`, so loop 2 is Neon City, Sunset Dunes, Sky Fortress, Magma Isle (Omega back on magma), loop 3 meadow, peaks, neon, dunes, loop 4 sky, magma, meadow, peaks, and round again. Bosses still rotate by stage number. Everything derives from the stage number, so co-op needs no new messages.
- **Audio**: ambience `amb_neon` / `amb_sky` and the reverb room, footsteps (neon metal, sky grass) and music now follow the actual biome id (`biomeAudio(stage)` uses `biomeForStage`). New music tracks: Neon City (minor, break beat, saw bass, 124 bpm) and Sky Fortress (mixolydian, bells, four-on-the-floor, 132 bpm), in the jukebox from stage 5 / 7.

## Key numbers and tuning
- `windTuning` (src/game/wind.ts): base 1.4 m/s², gust +5 (peak 6.4), period 11 s, gust 3.4 s, direction wanders ±0.45 rad, `playerBullets` 0.55, `enemyBullets` 1, `enemyPush` 0.38 (light enemies drift up to ~2.4 m/s in a gust), `lightXp` 2.
- `biomeFxTuning` (src/render/biomeFx.ts): vents puff 2.6 s then rest 2.5-6 s, a puff every 0.06 s, 1.5 s life; holograms glitch about every 5-6 s (off with REDUCE FLASHING); animation only within 40 m of the camera.
- Neon City: `night: 0.62` (always this dark when NIGHT STAGES is on; `&day` turns it off), `dusk: 0` (no sunset shift), `enemyTint #f0d4ff`, `eyeColor #5ef2ff`, grade saturation 1.12. Sky Fortress: `dusk: 0.6`.
- Perf (`npm run bench`, SwiftShader, heavy scene with 200 swarm + 500 bullets): meadow render 0.67 ms / sim 0.24 ms / 162 draw calls; Neon City 0.70 / 0.25 / 177; Sky Fortress 0.72 / 0.26 / 164. Allocation ~10-11 MB/s in all three.

## How it works (files)
- `src/data/biomes.ts`: the two defs; new optional fields `kind` ('neon' | 'sky'), `night`, `dusk`, `enemyTint`, `eyeColor`, `edge.shape` ('block' parapets/battlements), `sea.y`, tree style 'lamp'; `BIOME_BY_ID`, `FIRST_LOOP`, `LATER_ROTATION`, pure `biomeIdForStage`, `biomeOverride` (test links).
- `src/render/biomeTextures.ts` (new): 17 pixel textures for the two biomes and `biomeTex(key)`, which reads either set (src/render/textures.ts untouched). BiomeDef texture keys accept both.
- `src/world/fieldArena.ts`: neon/sky branches inside the existing generators (older biomes consume the RNG exactly as before, so their layouts are unchanged); `skyGaps` / `gapRects` (pure), cliffs generated per tile edge (shallow so chasms show the clouds), bridges, islets, skyline. New `Arena.deco` (`ArenaDeco`: lights, holos, vents, flags, airships, gaps); only Neon City and Sky Fortress arenas have it.
- `src/render/biomeFx.ts` (new): holograms, steam puffs (one InstancedMesh, 160 slots, `commit`), flag cloth vertex waves, airships. Driven from `updateLook`.
- `src/game/wind.ts` (new): pure `gustAt` / `windAt`, `sampleWind` (host: director clock; client: `duration - netTimeLeft`, then local during the boss) and the host step `updateWind` (installed on Game, called after `updateArena` in `update()`). Registers 'fall' in `ENV_SOURCES` / `ARENA_SOURCE_NAMES` at load.
- `src/world/collision.ts`: `AABB.gap`. `src/core/spatialHash.ts`: `StaticBoxGrid.hitSolid` (skips gaps). `src/game/systems/statuses.ts`: the bullet-vs-wall check uses `hitSolid` (one line).
- `src/render/biomeLook.ts`: grades for neon/sky, eye colour per biome, `dusk` factor, `currentBiome()`.
- `src/game/systems/look.ts`: builds the biome dressing on a new arena, sets the enemy tint, neon night (`max` of the stage's night and `biome.night`), neon lights into the light picker, wind into ambience/flags/steam, gust sound.
- `src/game/enemies.ts`: `Enemies.setTint(color)` (multiplies every batch's `uColor`; new batches pick it up).
- `src/game/ambience.ts`: weathers 'neon' (dust) and 'wind' (streaks driven by `Ambience.wind`), neon rain chance 0.6, softer rain tint in the city.
- `src/audio/mix.ts`, `ambienceAudio.ts` (rooms + loop ids), `music/composition.ts` (two tracks, `biomeTrack` by biome id), `systems/worldFx.ts` (dust colours), `game.ts` (3 lines: import, field, call).
- Tests: `tests/biomes.test.ts` (rotation, completeness, gap connectivity and rect cover, wind determinism and gust rate, seeded arenas for both biomes, wind on a solo game, chasm falls, host/client chasms and wind agreement). `tests/sound.test.ts` and `tests/music.test.ts` expectations moved to the new rotation (stage 5 is neon, 14 tracks).
- `scripts/bench.mjs`: `heavy` now forces `&biome=meadow` (stage 5 used to be the meadow, keep baselines comparable); new `--scene neon` / `--scene sky`.

## Test links
- `?test&biome=neon` / `?test&biome=sky` (any biome id; local only) and `at=holo`, `at=vent`, `at=sign`, `at=flag`, `at=gap` to start in front of that piece. Natural stages: `?test&stage=5` is Neon City, `?test&stage=7` the Sky Fortress.

## Risks and loose ends
- Chasms are colliders with `gap: true`: everything that walks (heroes, enemies, spawns, chests, the portal check) treats them as walls; only the bullet check was switched to `hitSolid`. Beam/area weapons never checked walls, so they're unaffected. Rex's charge and the Reaper ignore walls and can cross a chasm (they just hover over it). Enemy cover AI may "hide" behind a chasm edge.
- `npm run coop` plays stages 1-2 only (meadow, dunes); the Sky Fortress co-op path is covered in-process by tests/biomes.test.ts. The first `npm run coop` run failed once on its jump-pad check (the host was in a level-up menu); the rerun passed, so it looks flaky and unrelated.
- Neon City's darkness follows the NIGHT STAGES setting: with it off, the city uses its own dusky light (still readable, less moody).
- Gust timing on clients comes from the synced time-left; during the boss it runs on a local clock, so gust visuals may drift a little from the host (bullets are host-simulated, so gameplay is unaffected).
- Not done: a Neon City hazard (steam vents are cosmetic). Hero barks and boss lines don't mention the new areas.
