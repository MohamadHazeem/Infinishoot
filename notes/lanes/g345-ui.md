# Gates 3–5 UI readability

## Boss announcements

A warning immediately takes the centre slot. The active toast keeps its remaining reading time, newly arriving toasts join the existing FIFO, and an interrupted chest reveal resumes before the next toast. Boss entrances, finishers and cinematic title cards suppress those HUD messages. The queue retains its four-message limit, 1.2-second minimum reading interval under contention and 12-second expiry for stale waiting messages.

The original warning could survive its timer. Its animation scheduled the addition of the visibility class in a later animation frame, after the game had already removed it. The HUD now sets visibility synchronously and restarts an existing animation separately. At one second the fixture shows the boss warning. At four seconds the 2.2-second warning has expired and the queued toast has a clear reading interval.

The existing boss mechanic hint moves into the goals stack, including its RTL positioning, live translation and update lifetime. Challenges use the same stack's progress row, so their opening notification no longer repeats the goal in the centre. The goal sentence is localized separately from its progress and completion marker. The boss health bar still owns its phase and enrage lines. The warning registers as a fixed dock anchor.

## Level-up and pause

Each card shows its name, one-line description, stats and tags with up to one context chip. A fusion or evolution recipe wins first. A ready evolution identifies its base weapon without sending partner flags. The presentation reads that weapon’s recipe and marks both owned components. Next comes a set, preferring a newly activated tier, then a hero path, then an armour counter. The tooltip includes the complete description, recipe and owned-part marks, replacement or Overdrive text if applicable, all set and path effects, and the armour hint. The existing slot ribbons, rarities, selection prompts and rolls remain.

Keyboard and D-pad inspection follows the selected card even when the pointer is parked on another card. Mouse hover works independently. The detached pick animation drops the tooltip. Compact reels use matching 48 px sprites, clipping windows and stops. The pause build's equipment badges can take controller focus and show fusion components and armour counters alongside the existing evolution, set and hero-path panels.
