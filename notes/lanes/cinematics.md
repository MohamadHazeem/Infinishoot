# Lane: cinematics (wave 2: sky-fall transition, painterly ending, legendary reveal, jackpot chest, flawless reward, playable title)

## What players get
- **Sky-fall transition** (replaces the 0.8 s portal fade). Stepping into the portal: the hero leaps up in an arc and dives into the ring (0.7 s, the camera leans in on the portal), the screen whites out in cloud, and the next stage is built behind it. Then a freefall from 60 m: the camera hangs over the hero's back with a wide lens and turns slowly, the hero is in a skydive pose (belly down, arms and legs spread, fluttering), chunky pixel cloud puffs stream past from the middle of the screen out (never parked over the hero), with wind streaks. Under the fall the new arena **assembles**: the island base rises out of the sea, the ground tiles drop in and grow into place in a wave outward from the landing spot, then props, walls, trees and ruins rise out of the ground. The hero tucks the legs just before the ground, lands (dust burst, ring, thump, rumble, a small shake) in a superhero crouch and the camera hands back to the follow camera. 3.6 s (SHORT 2.0 s). Any key, click or pad button skips (after 0.5 s). The HUD and crosshair hide while it plays; the STAGE N banner shows over the fall as before.
  - Golden route (see flawless): the clouds turn sunset gold and orange.
- **Painterly ending** after the final boss (every loop's Omega): about 1.6 s into VICTORY (after the K.O. finisher, VICTORY! and the chain-reaction fireworks) a sequence of painted panels plays, each a canvas painting made of thousands of seeded brush strokes over a watercolour wash, with paper grain and fibres multiplied on top and a torn, bleeding edge, slowly pushing in (Ken Burns) and cross-fading:
  1. "THE OVERLORD FALLS." Omega (a horned dome with one great cracked eye, in its own colours) tips into a storm-sunset sea in a burst of light rays, hull shards flying, smoke and spray; the hero (in the player's colours) on a rock in the foreground, cannon raised.
  2. "ONE BY ONE, THE ISLANDS GO QUIET." Dawn over a calm sea: islands paling with distance, soft clouds, birds, sun glitter on the water.
  3. "BUT OUT PAST THE HORIZON, SOMETHING STIRS..." The hero from behind on a windswept cliff, looking out at a golden evening, a faint spire with a glint on the horizon.
  4. A paper card with a brushed blot: **THE END?**, "LOOP 2 BEGINS · EVERYTHING HITS HARDER" and a credits line ("INFINISHOOT · THANKS FOR PLAYING · v...").
  Music: the beds drop out for 1.6 s at the start, then a roar, a jingle, an ominous sting and the legendary fanfare as the panels change. 12.7 s (SHORT 7.2 s), skippable after 0.5 s. Then the shop and loop 2 as before.
- **Legendary reveal**: the first legendary of a run (and any legendary this machine has never revealed) gained in play gets a short cinematic: the music drops out, letterbox bars, a column of light (tinted with the item's colour) comes down on the hero, the item's pixel icon spins down the beam, and on touchdown a flash, burst, ring and the legendary fanfare ("Legendary!") with a title card: LEGENDARY WEAPON! / LEGENDARY ITEM! / EVOLUTION! / FUSION!, the name in the item's colour and what it does. The camera pushes in and back out. Counts: legendary auto weapons (Starfall), legendary items (Vampire Fang), fusions, loadout evolutions (the legendary fork card), special evolutions and auto-weapon partner evolutions, from a level-up pick (fusions and evolutions are picks), a map chest or a jackpot. Only those gain moments count (the second after a level-up menu closes, a chest opening, the jackpot's reels): anything that appears otherwise (debug grants, test-link kits, scripted changes in other systems) joins the baseline silently, and several legendaries gained at once reveal only the first (the rest count as seen). Solo: 2.7 s (SHORT 1.5 s), the world pauses. Online: 1.9 s on that player's screen only, the world keeps running and the HUD stays. Skippable.
- **Jackpot chest**: 7% of map chest spawns (at most one a stage) are a golden chest instead: gold with white bands and a red jewel, bobbing and sparkling under a light pillar, "A GOLDEN CHEST APPEARED!" and a cackle. Walking in opens a full-screen slot machine: a red cabinet with a JACKPOT marquee, three big white reels of pixel symbols (7, star, clover, crown, chest, gem) spinning and stopping left to right with ticks and landing clunks, 44 bulbs chasing around the whole screen edge (flashing together on the win), then the payout: **JACKPOT!** a pile of gold (30 + 12 per stage; coins fly from the reels to the HUD gold counter) or **TRIPLE TREASURE!** three chest items on the payline, listed by name. 3.8 s (SHORT 2.1 s). The first skip lands the reels, the second closes. Solo: the world holds. Online: on the opener's screen only, they're untouchable for 2.4 s, teammates get a "<NAME> HIT THE JACKPOT!" toast. A rewind can't take the payout back.
- **Flawless stage**: clearing a stage without anyone getting hit: confetti from both bottom corners, a big **FLAWLESS!** stamp ("NO HITS TAKEN · A GOLDEN PORTAL WILL OPEN"), the announcer's "Perfect!" (captioned "Flawless!" when the line can't play), a jingle. When the portal opens, a **golden portal** opens beside it ("FLAWLESS BONUS: TAKE THE GOLDEN PORTAL!"). Taking it is the golden route: gold clouds on the way down, and on landing "GOLDEN ROUTE!": +15 +5/stage gold for every hero, a treasure chest next to each hero, and a 35% chance of a relic for everyone. Endless (no portal): the bonus comes right away. The normal portal still works.
- **Playable title screen**: the attract demo behind the title hands its hero to you the moment you move or aim it (WASD / left stick, right stick, a click on the backdrop, RT, X, dash). A hint at the bottom right says how ("WASD TAKE THE HERO"), then "YOU'RE PLAYING · NOTHING IS SAVED · MENU: ↑↓ ENTER". After 5 s without input the bot takes over again. Your shots are heard softly. Nothing is saved or counted (the demo's profile lock stays on).
  - **Input sharing on the title**: the hero gets the move keys (minus any arrow keys), the left stick, mouse aim, clicks on the backdrop (not on a button), the right stick, RT, X / right click, Space / Shift / LB / LT (dash), Q / E, C, V. The menu keeps the arrow keys, D-pad, Enter and A (and the mouse on its buttons). While the demo listens, MenuNav ignores W/A/S/D, Space and the left stick, and the hero ignores the arrows, D-pad, A, B, View and Start.
- **SETTINGS > CINEMATICS**: FULL (default), SHORT, OFF. OFF keeps the old 0.8 s fade, skips the ending and the reveals, and shows jackpots and FLAWLESS as toasts (the golden chest, its payout, the golden portal and its bonus still happen). REDUCE FLASHING or the OS reduced-motion switch plays the short versions without flashes or shakes.

## How it works
- src/game/cinematics.ts: tuning and pure rules (timelines, curves, cloud cover, assembly delays, rise offsets, ending frames, jackpot plans, reel scroll, bulb chase, confetti paths, the reveal policy and its per-machine memory `cine.revealed.v1` in localStorage through src/core/storage.ts).
- src/game/systems/cinematics.ts (Game methods): `cineStep` at the top of `Game.update`, right after the time lane's holds and before the client split: takes skips, runs the host rules (stage clears via `bossesRun`, the ending trigger, the golden portal and its entry, the golden bonus, golden chests), polls the local hero's legendaries every 6 steps (the first look of a run is the baseline, so starting kits and test-link items don't reveal), advances the overlays' clocks, and returns true while a solo cinematic holds the world (the update then ends like the time lane's holds). `skyfallTransition` is the first line of `Game.updateTransition`: the host's sky fall runs on `modeT`, loads the stage at the cut, ends in `fight` with `modeT = 0.8` (what the old fade left, for systems that wait on it). `jackpotRoll` is called from `updateChestSpawns` before a normal chest is added. `renderCinematics` runs every frame after the client fade block in render: camera override (blended from and back to the rig's own camera), poses, the assembly, 3D pieces, overlays.
- src/game/cineWorld.ts: `GoldPortal`, `JackpotChests` (plain meshes, at most a few), `RevealBeam`, `ArenaAssembly` (moves the arena group's own children and ground vertices, then writes every original value back), `poseSkydive` / `poseLeap` / `poseLand` (applied after the player's own animation each frame).
- src/ui/cinematicsUi.ts (CSS injected): the cloud canvas (320x180, upscaled pixelated; one 32x20 puff sprite per palette, scaled crisp), the ending, the reveal card and dropping icon, the jackpot cabinet, bulbs and coins, the confetti canvas and stamp, the title hint. src/ui/painterly.ts paints the four panels (seeded strokes, wash, paper, deckle edge); a panel is painted when needed (the next one during the current one), ~once per ending.
- src/game/titlePlay.ts: `TitleInput` (the real input through the title's sharing rules, the same shape as Input for the hero code), `navLock` (read by MenuNav), `titlePlayTick` (who drives the demo hero). src/game/systems/attract.ts steps the demo with it when the player has the hero.

## Co-op / protocol (backwards compatible: old peers ignore the new field and event)
- Host -> clients, snapshot field `cn` (generic snapshot path, null when nothing is up): `jc` golden chests `[id, x, z, facing, openT]`, `gp` the golden portal `[x, z]` or 0, `gr` 1 while the team rides the golden route.
- Effects bus `['C', kind, hero, x, z, data]` (generic fxCodec path, team-wide): `flawless` (data = the subtitle), `ending` (hero = the loop), `jackpot` (hero = the opener, data = the reel symbols and payout lines as JSON). Every machine plays its own part: the opener's screen spins the reels, everyone else gets a toast.
- The host decides everything that matters (golden chest rolls and payouts on the seeded RNG, the flawless check, the golden portal and its bonus, sky-fall timing). Cinematics never hold the world online: the reveal and jackpot play on one screen while the game runs (the jackpot opener gets i-frames instead), the ending sits over the shop (its key presses are swallowed so they can't buy), and the sky fall plays where the game already waits between stages, each machine on its own clock: a client starts its fall when the host's `transition` arrives, waits in the clouds until its own `stage` message has built the arena, and lands quickly when the host is already fighting. The host's CINEMATICS setting sets the transition length (like the finisher's hold); a client that skips or has it OFF sees the old fade until the host's stage starts.

## Unfinished / risky
- **Nothing here was played by a person.** Timings (3.6 s fall, 12.7 s ending, 2.7 s reveal, 3.8 s reels) are first guesses.
- **Reveals after a pick only**: a legendary gained through some other future path (a new shop item, an event reward) won't reveal unless it opens a gain window (`debugGainWindow(g)` is the hook; call it, or set `cineState(g).gainUntil = g.stepN + 90`).
- **The golden chest has no edge pointer or minimap dot** (its light pillar and the toast are the cue; the chest pointer and minimap only know `game.chests`).
- **God-mode bot runs are always flawless** (no damage is taken), so `botRun` god reports now include a golden route every stage (+15 +5/stage gold and a chest per hero, sometimes a relic). The bot also prefers the golden portal (src/game/autopilot.ts). Mortal runs rarely clear flawless.
- **The ending's music** is the existing victory cue plus `music.silence` and sfx stings; there is no dedicated ending track (the SOUND lane could add one; `s.end` is non-null while it plays).
- **The reveal history** is per machine (localStorage `cine.revealed.v1`), not in the save file, so it isn't exported with SAVE TO FILE and doesn't need a save version bump. The first legendary of each run reveals regardless.
- **Arena assembly** moves the arena group's own children and the flat ground meshes' vertices; other lanes' pieces outside that group (barrels, chests, arena toys, ambience tufts, puddles and ponds, biome decor drawn elsewhere) simply appear. The detection is generic (flat meshes in groups of 4 or 6 vertices are tiles; meshes under the ground are the island base); a biome with a different ground structure just doesn't animate that part. Everything is written back exactly when it ends or is skipped.
- Online the client falls on its own clock: if the host's `stage` message is late, the client stays in the clouds until it arrives (bounded by the host starting the stage); a client whose host has CINEMATICS OFF sees the old fade.
- The rig's ground ring (readability lane) rides along under the falling hero.
- **A coupling worth knowing**: tests/gameplay.test.ts's Powder Keg / Nova Heart check failed while this lane built its (invisible) golden-portal group into the scene at the first step; building the golden portal and the reveal beam only when first needed fixed it, and also golden chest rolls are seeded from the run seed + stage + spawn number instead of drawn from the game's RNG stream (so the rest of a run's randomness is the same as without golden chests). I didn't find why an extra scene object changed that test's outcome (nothing gameplay-side walks the scene); the test looks sensitive to world state.
- `npm run bench` (heavy scene) on a machine shared with four other lanes (1.1 fps SwiftShader): 4.96 ms per sim step, 129 draw calls average (wave 1 reported 3.97-4.2 ms and 122-137 in the same conditions); noisy. The lane adds no meshes in normal play (the golden portal, beam and golden chests are built when first needed); per step it polls the local hero's legendaries every 6 steps (a dozen entries at most).
- `npm run coop` not run (no PeerJS broker here); covered by the in-process host + client test. Not checked: real GPUs (SwiftShader only), touch devices on the playable title, the Electron build.
- The playable title's mouse fire listens on `window` for clicks that aren't on menu elements; a menu added later that isn't a button/`data-nav`/input/label/link would fire the demo hero's gun when clicked.
