## Capture and checks

Each controlled clip uses seed 42 and a six-second warmup. The driver aims or releases the real weapon, fires the special, earns XP and picks an offer, crosses the boss health gate, resolves lethal damage, or dashes around an incoming hostile shot. It stages 16 targets for firing, six high-HP Rhinos for the separate flinch test, and 54 targets plus the starting enemies for the streak. Baseline and final use the same staging. Particle scatter, AI timing and incidental kills can vary. Each clip samples 20 frames at about 12 fps except the streak (26) and boss kill (49). The WebAudio mix was captured at the game output before browser playback. Open the individual MP4s to compare the original sounds.

## Integration and decisions

- `src/data/weapons.ts` and `src/data/juice.ts` hold feel tuning. `src/game/juiceCombat.ts` scopes hit feedback around the existing damage resolver and handles heavy hurt and low-life dash. `src/game/juiceVitals.ts` tracks a local HUD pulse. `src/game/systems/anim.ts` adds corpse accents. `src/game/vfx.ts` records resolved hit geometry and replays presentation from existing flourish and sound records. `src/audio/sfx.ts` reuses the recorded death, bell and voice clips and adds the low-heartbeat overtone. `tests/juice.test.ts` exercises tuning, damage, compact effect encoding and replay, slow-motion gates, and the new-run regression. This file is the lane decision log.
- Shared `src/ui/hud.ts` gained one import, one pulse instance and one call in its existing vignette path. Shared `src/game/game.ts` wires the damage wrapper, VFX reward/hurt callbacks and the low-life dash. Its new-run hook also clears two stale arcade fields. On the Nov 21 daily seed, leaving an arcade round alive carried its holster flag and timer into a subsequent standard run. That prevented the new hero from firing and skipped the first route choice. Smoke failed twice and a direct reproduction on 717b3ed confirmed the cause. Resetting both fields at the run boundary fixes shooting and routing for subsequent runs.
- Use the existing source-coloured labels, sound records, compact geometry records and enemy squash snapshot. This keeps save and network formats stable. No new player-facing text. Calm mode and the existing shake, sound, voice, crowd and damage-number settings remain in control of the corresponding systems.
- Known limits from review: very tightly packed, simultaneous kills can still overlap labels before the pressure governor engages. The existing boss-finisher caption renders in English in the Arabic capture. The corpse-based streak ring needs an actual corpse event. Hardware GPU frame time and the stock local Chromium launchers remain unverified here.
