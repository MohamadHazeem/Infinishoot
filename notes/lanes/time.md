# Lane: time (rewind, last stand, bullet-time dodges, death replay, clutch revives)

## What players get
- **Time rewind** (X, pad D-pad left, touch REWIND; rebindable in SETTINGS > CONTROLS). Once per stage, rewinds the last 5 seconds. Time runs backwards on screen for 1.1 s (a VHS look: scanlines, tracking bars, a washed-out picture, "◀◀ REWIND" with a "-3.6 S" counter and a timecode), then a "PLAY ▶" flash and the world is put back: your position, life, dash charges, special and ultimate meters, gold and gears; every enemy's position, life, statuses and AI timers (enemies killed in those 5 s come back, ones spawned since leave); every bullet and pickup; telegraphed strikes; score, kills, combo, XP and the stage clock. You get 1.2 s of invulnerability on landing. A new REWIND tank sits next to DASH / special / ULT and fills as history builds up (it needs 1 s of history), with its button prompt on top; it greys out once used.
  - A rewind never crosses a level-up or shop menu, an opened chest, the boss arriving or a player joining or leaving (the history starts over there), so it can't undo a pick or loot a chest twice.
  - Running bomb waves and ultimates stop (their meters came back). Other weapon internals keep going as they are (mines, drones, discs, cooldowns).
- **Co-op: TEAM REWIND.** The rewind belongs to the team (one per stage). A press opens a vote: "PLAYER WANTS TO REWIND · 1/2 READY · [X] AGREE" with a 6 s timer (and a toast for teammates). Once everyone still standing has pressed it, the host rewinds the whole team: the world holds on the host for the 1.1 s playback, so every screen plays it backwards (each client's own hero follows the host's playback), and every hero lands where it was 5 s ago, downed teammates who were up back then included. Nobody else agreeing in time: "TEAM REWIND: NOT EVERYONE AGREED", the charge is kept.
- **Last stand.** A killing blow while the team had a x50 combo going (the best combo of the last 3 s: hits break the combo, so the killing blow usually lands right after a break) doesn't kill you: time freezes for a beat (solo; online the picture holds briefly), a heartbeat sting, red screen frame and "LAST STAND · KILL 30!" with a counter and a 5 s bar. You're untouchable for those 5 s. 30 kills: SURVIVED!, 35% life back, the combo restored, a little style. Out of time: OUT OF TIME, and the blow lands (then the usual down / revive / Second Wind / pet rescue rules). Once per stage (tunable to once per run). Co-op: per hero, judged on the host. Not in PvP or the training field.
- **Bullet-time dodge.** In boss and mini-boss fights a near miss drops into slow motion with the camera swinging around you for ~1.2 s and "CLOSE CALL!" between letterbox bars (+30 style): a strike zone landing within 1.4 m outside you, a boss landing a jump slam just beside you, a charging boss rushing past within 1.1 m, a bullet ring grazing past (enough enemy bullets inside a 0.6 m band around you), or a perfect dash. At most one per 12 s per hero. Solo: real slow motion (35% speed for 0.9 s) if SKILL SLOW-MO is on; online: the camera swing and effects only, on the dodging player's screen, the shared simulation never slows. The swing follows DYNAMIC CAMERA.
- **Death replay.** When the run ends, after the fall, the last 1.8 s before the killing blow play back over 3.2 s (x0.6) from a low, orbiting camera close on the hero, with cinema bars, a blinking REPLAY tag and a speed readout; the hero falls again at the moment of the hit; then it holds 0.6 s with a "so close" card: "KING BOPPER HAD 12% LIFE LEFT", "2 SECONDS FROM THE BOSS", "4 KILLS SHORT OF A LAST STAND", "3 KILLS FROM LEVEL 20", "1,200 POINTS FROM YOUR BEST", "A x87 COMBO ENDED HERE", "YOUR REWIND WAS STILL CHARGED" (the closest one wins), else "LASTED 2:31 · 312 KILLS". Any button skips it; then the normal game-over flow. SETTINGS > DEATH REPLAY turns it off. Co-op: it plays on every screen when the whole team is down, from the host's recording, on the last hero to fall (clients can hide it on their own screen).
- **Clutch revive** (co-op). A revive that finishes in dire straits (a rescuer on 35% life or less, 10+ enemies within 6 m of the downed hero, or with 3+ players the rescuer was the last one standing) plays a 1.7 s moment on every screen: cinema bars, a pixel panel of two gloved hands (in the two heroes' colours) reaching and clasping with a burst, "CLUTCH SAVE! / ACE PULLED BUDDY BACK!", a chord, and (for the two heroes and anyone within 20 m) the camera closing in on both. Never slows the simulation online. At most one per 20 s.

## How it works
- src/game/timeline.ts: a ring buffer of 58 frames (5.6 s at 10 Hz) recorded at the top of a step while fighting, on solo and host machines. Each frame is typed arrays that only grow (team numbers, up to 4 heroes, every enemy's 65 fields + uid, bullets, pickups, strikes) plus intern tables for enemy defs, elite mods and state strings. `restore(frame, 'visual' | 'full', next?, k?)` moves everything drawn (and reconciles which enemies exist), or puts everything back; the replay blends positions toward the next frame (bullets extrapolate along their velocity). Enemies killed since come back as fresh pool objects given their old uid (squad links and co-op mirrors line up; uids stay unique among active enemies, which the snapshot codec's sorted uid deltas need). Bosses and mini-bosses are never despawned or respawned.
- src/game/systems/time.ts: Game methods. `timeStep` runs at the top of `Game.update` (before the client split): holds (the rewind playback and the replay stop the world: `update()` returns after `input.endStep()` and `hostTick`, like the boss finisher's hold), recording, history barriers, the rewind button, the vote, last stands, near misses, the death replay start. `lastStand` (from hurtHero), `timeKill` (from killEnemy), `clutchRevive` (from updateRevives, before the high-five heal so the rescuer's low life counts). `renderTime` runs right after the camera rig in render: it overrides the camera for the cinematic beats (orbit, pitch and distance blended from the rig's own framing, so moves start and end seamlessly) and drives the HUD.
- src/ui/timeHud.ts: the REWIND tank (#meters), VHS overlay, letterbox, replay overlay and card, LAST STAND panel and red frame, clutch panel with pixel hands, vote panel; CSS injected from the module (Sweetie 16, pixel frames, steps()), panels follow UI SCALE with `zoom: var(--ui)`. Body classes `tm-vhs`, `tm-stand`, `tm-replay` tint the game canvas with CSS filters; `tm-hold` / `tm-cine` hide the reticle and (replay) the in-game HUD.
- src/game/timeAudio.ts: synthesized cues through `sfx.play` (rewind whir, PLAY clunk, last-stand heartbeat, win/fail, bullet-time whoosh, clutch chord, tape stop, vote blip); music goes silent for the rewind and the stand.

## Co-op / protocol (backwards compatible: old peers ignore the new field and message)
- Client -> host: `{t:'rw'}` (the rewind button).
- Host -> clients: snapshot field `tm`: `m` [team charge spent, history 0..1], `e` time events since that client's last snapshot (`[seq, kind, hero, x, z, extra]`: rw rewind start, re rewind end (one per hero, x/z = where it lands, extra = dash charges), ls/lw/lf last stand start/won/failed, bt bullet time (x/z = the attack), cr clutch revive (extra = rescuer), rp replay start (extra = length), vo vote, no refused (extra = reason)), `h` [hold kind 1 rewind / 2 replay, progress], `p` the client's own position during a rewind playback, `so` the replay's two lines, `s` that client's last stand [kills, need, time 0..1], `v` the vote [yes, need, time 0..1, caller, you agreed]. The snapshot codec's generic path carries it; unchanged parts cost nothing.
- Clients hold (no local movement or input) while `h` says a rewind or replay runs, release if the host goes quiet for 1 s, and teleport their own hero on their `re` event. The host also sets each remote hero's last input position to where it landed, so it doesn't drift back before the client's next input.

- Screenshots (listed above) checked: VHS rewind mid-playback (enemies pulled back toward where they spawned, counter -3.6 S), last stand panel and red frame, bullet-time swing with letterbox and CLOSE CALL!, replay close-up from the low orbit, the so-close card over the fallen hero, clutch panel with the hands, the vote panel.
- Cost: `Timeline.record` 0.17 ms per frame with 300 enemies, 500 bullets and 1000 pickups (10 times a second, so ~0.03 ms per step on average), 5 KB of heap growth over 2000 records (no per-frame garbage); a visual restore 0.6 ms (holds only); memory ~7.9 MB for the 58 frames at that load (~3 MB at a typical 200/300/400). `npm run bench` ran (heavy scene, 15 s) on a machine shared with four other lanes, so its absolute numbers are noisy: 4.2 ms per sim step, 122 draw calls (unchanged: the lane draws no meshes), 10 MB/s allocated.
- Bot (Normal, Rookie, mortal, seeds 4-9, 700 s): last stands opened in 3 of 6 runs (combos 121, 146, 102 at the blow) and the bot won all three; without the 3 s combo memory none of the 4 measured lethal blows had a x50 combo (8-41).

## Unfinished / risky
- **Not rewound:** weapon internals (mines on the ground, drones, discs, cooldowns), specials other than the Bomb that are mid-cast, stage events and challenges, the director's spawn timers (an elite spawned in the window comes again a cycle later), barrels, arena toys and boss arena twists, pets, the TEAM meter, the style meter and Mod counters, the RNG. Mirror bots re-copy their weapon. A hero downed at the target frame but standing now stays up.
- **Online rewinds stop everyone's world for 1.1 s** (by agreement). Clients learn of the hold one snapshot late (~50 ms + ping); there's no client-side prediction. Not tested between two real machines (`npm run coop` can't reach the PeerJS broker here); covered by the in-process test.
- **Co-op death replay** is the host's recording of the last hero to fall; a client's own earlier down isn't replayed. A client's SKIP only hides it on that screen.
- **Game over comes ~5 s after death** with the replay (0.8 s fall + 3.8 s replay + the old 0.4 s), skippable. tests/onboarding.test.ts waited 200 steps for game over; it now waits 360. Other wave-2 tests that kill the hero and expect game over within ~3 s should skip the replay (`settings.deathReplay = false`) or step longer.
- **The bot rewinds** (once per stage, under 40% life), so `botRun` balance reports now include rewinds.
- The last-stand hero blinks for its 5 s (it's i-frames). CSS filters on the game canvas during holds and stands cost a compositing pass (short moments only).
- The rewind button is D-pad left; the ping wheel or a later lane may want it.
- Nothing here was played by a person. The numbers (x50 combo, 30 kills in 5 s, 12 s bullet-time cooldown) are first guesses; the bot won every last stand it got from stage 2 on.
