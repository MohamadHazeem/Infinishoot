# Lane: graphics (wave 1: destruction, anime look, water, fog, framing, customisation)

## What players get
- **Props really break.** Crates, crate stacks, barrels, totems, the toppling pillars and the cover Rex smashes (ruin walls, ruin pillars, rocks, trees) split into chunks cut from their own box: crates into textured planks, barrels into red and yellow staves, pillars into drums along their length when they hit the ground, cover in its own colours (material colour x texture average x vertex colours of its merged triangles). Chunks fly away from the blow, tumble, bounce, slide to a stop, tip onto a flat face, pile on top of each other (a coarse pile height map), lie there 7-10 s, then shrink and sink away. Outlined like enemies.
- **ANIME LOOK** (SETTINGS > ANIME LOOK, default off): ink outlines on everything from the depth buffer (walls, trees, props, enemies, heroes; weather streaks are skipped), hull outlines twice as thick, two flat hard-edged cel bands, manga speed lines along your dash (clear around the hero), and 2-frame black-and-white impact frames on your big crits (a crit 2.4x your recent average, at least 60), heavy hit-stops and boss kills (0.9 s cooldown; never with REDUCE FLASHING, which also halves the speed lines).
- **Reflective water and puddles.** Every dynamic light of the frame (explosion flashes, muzzle flashes, bullets, neon signs and street lamps, lanterns, lava, the portal) is mirrored in ponds, in the sea near the shore and in puddles in real time: a glint where the line from the camera to the light's mirror image crosses the water, stretched toward the camera like real water reflections and broken up by pixel-stepped ripples. Neon City's roofs get 90 seeded puddles, rainy stages 60 (darker wet patches with a faint sky/neon sheen).
- **Volumetric ground fog on dark stages** (night stages, Neon City, anything another lane forces with `fogForce`): stacked layers of drifting pixel mist that follow the camera, lit from inside by the lights (a lit dome around every lantern, explosion and sign, banded like the cel lighting), legs and bullets wade through it; on MEDIUM+ the two brightest lights also throw radial light shafts (screen-space scattering) that dark enemies cut streaks through.
- **Crowd-aware camera.** When the screen is nearly empty the camera eases lower and a little closer (58 -> 47 deg, 10% closer) over a few seconds; as enemies fill the view (4 -> 22 in view) or a boss is up it rises back quickly. Off with DYNAMIC CAMERA, fades out as you wheel-zoom out (1.15 -> 1.6), capped at half in co-op, dropped during the kill-cam. Aim stays exact (the cursor is ray-cast through the camera that is drawn; unit-tested at full tilt).
- **EXTRAS > CUSTOMISE.** Armour, body and accent colours from the Sweetie 16 palette (or SKIN to keep the skin's), an accessory (antenna, cap, scarf with a fluttering tail, crown; built from primitives on the rig, in the merged outline and x-ray, in photo mode) and a weapon skin (Aqua Pulse, Toxic, Ember, Royal Gold, Prism) that recolours your projectiles, their tails, flame/shock/glow trails and the light they cast. The camera moves in on your hero while the screen is open, changes apply live. The HUD face and portraits use the colours. Saved in settings (`settings.look`, a 5-character code); co-op teammates get it in `hello` / `start` and see your hat, colours and shots (in co-op the armour keeps your player colour, as with skins). Enemy shots never change, no skin uses (or comes near) the enemy-bullet magenta, COLOUR-BLIND BULLETS still applies to enemy shots.

## Knobs (debug panel > "Destruction, anime, water & fog")
- `shatterTuning` (src/render/shatter.ts): pool 260 plain + 130 crate chunks (ring buffers), gravity 24, bounce 0.35, slide kept 0.65, friction 5/s, rest below 0.35 m/s, lie 7 s + 0-3 s, sink 1.2 s, pile cell 0.55 m, pile max 1.4 m, launch 5.5 m/s x power, up 5 m/s; pieces at most 1.15 m (`maxPiece`); LOW keeps half the pieces.
- `animeTuning` (src/game/systems/graphics.ts): ink 1.5 px at 270p, threshold 0.05 (relative Laplacian of 1/depth), fade-out 90 m; hull outline x2; 2 cel bands with 0.02 softness; speed lines linger 0.16 s; impact 2 frames, 0.9 s cooldown, crit x2.4 average and >= 60, hit-stop >= 0.075 s.
- `reflectTuning` (src/render/reflections.ts): strength 1.15, glint 0.55 m + 6% of the light radius, stretch 3.2, ripple 0.22 m; puddles darken 0.32, sheen 0.34; 90 on Neon City, 60 in rain, 0.9-2.4 m.
- `volumeFogTuning` (src/render/volumeFog.ts): 3 layers (LOW+ 2, LOW 0) from 0.18 m every 0.42 m, 70 x 56 m sheet, density 0.15, scatter 0.5, reach 1.1x the light radius; `gfxTuning`: fog from night 0.3 (full at 0.85), shafts 0.75, reach 0.55 screens.
- `cineTuning` (src/game/cineCam.ts): pitch drop 11 deg, 10% closer, full tilt at <= 4 enemies in view, none from 22, settle 0.35/s, pull-up 2.2/s, wheel fade 1.15-1.6, co-op cap 0.5.

## Quality
- LOW: no fog, no shafts, no water glints on ponds or the sea (puddles still darken and glint: they are cheap), half the destruction pieces, hull outlines off as before (the ANIME LOOK ink pass still runs: 5 depth fetches a pixel).
- LOW+: 2 fog layers, pond and puddle glints, no sea mirror, no shafts. MEDIUM/HIGH: 3 layers, sea mirror, shafts. AUTO moves with the governor.

## How it works (for maintainers)
- `src/game/systems/graphics.ts` `updateGraphics(game, frameDt)` (one call in systems/render.ts after `worldFx`, so this frame's lights are picked): watches barrels / props / pillars / totems for state changes (every machine sees those: host simulation, client snapshots) and calls `Shatter.burst`; cover comes through `coverHooks.broke` in src/game/coverBreak.ts, called on every machine before the triangles collapse. Pillars: when the fall lands the pillar's `fallT` jumps past its lying time so Interactives stops drawing it and the drums take over.
- Ink edges, speed lines, impact frames and shafts are one GLSL chunk (src/render/graphicsPost.ts) called from the composite (src/render/postfx.ts) after grading; the scene target got a `DepthTexture` (src/render/renderer.ts). Cel softness is `retroGlobals.uCelSoft` (default 0.15 = the old look), hull width `outlineStyle.scale` (src/render/outlines.ts).
- Water: `WaterMirror` overlays (ponds from `ambience.surfaces.patches`, a sea plane, instanced puddles from `puddleSpots(seed)`), premultiplied blending that leaves the bloom mask alone. `createMirrorMaterial(kind, waterY)` + `mirrorUniforms` are exported for other water (the WORLD lane's flood water: put a plane at the water height with it).
- Fog: `VolumeFog` (one instanced draw for all layers), `fogForce.amount` for the eclipse / stealth night.
- Camera: `CameraRig.cine` (target) and `cineShown` (eased); additive in `update()` (pitch and distance only), so other lanes' camera moves layer on top.
- Customisation: src/data/customise.ts (palette, accessories, weapon skins, `encodeLook` / `decodeLook` / `cleanLook`), src/game/heroLooks.ts (`dressHero` from `Player.setSkin`, so every re-skin keeps it; `customSkin` for portraits; `shotColor` by bullet owner), src/render/accessories.ts (5 rig parts, 34 of the 40 the merged overlay supports), src/ui/customise.ts (screen, ANIME LOOK toggle, CSS injected). Bullets keep their real colour (looks, the codec); the drawn colour comes from `shotColor(owner, ...)` in `Bullets.render`. The bullet record now carries the owner in bits 4-5 of byte 11 (was free), so clients colour teammates' shots.
- Protocol: `look` (5 chars, `cleanLook` on receipt) in `hello`, the lobby entry and each `start` player (co-op and PvP). Older peers ignore it; missing = plain look.

## Unfinished / risky
- Not played by a person; everything checked in headless SwiftShader screenshots only. Not measured on a real GPU: the ink pass (5 depth fetches), shafts (20 scene fetches a pixel, MEDIUM+ on dark stages only), fog layers (3 overdraw layers of a light loop) and the water light loops are the new fill cost.
- Reflections mirror lights, not geometry: a pond doesn't show the hero or a tree upside down (that needs a screen copy and marching; out of budget). Glints only show where a light's mirror point lands on water, so a pond far from any light shows the sky lane's reflection only.
- The fog colour comes from the scene fog; on some night biomes it reads as a tinted haze over the whole field rather than low wisps. Tune `volumeFogTuning.density` / `gfxTuning.fogFrom` in play.
- Destruction is local per machine (no sync): the same props break everywhere, but pieces fly in different directions on each screen (random). Pieces don't collide with walls (they can pass through a nearby wall), only pile on each other.
- Pillar shattering sets `PropState.fallT` past its lying time from the graphics system (no edit to Interactives' render). If the pillar render logic changes, check `watchBreaks` in systems/graphics.ts.
- Impact frames hook `game.dmg.spawn` at runtime (like look.ts wraps `fx.ring`). Crit size is relative to a running average, so builds with uniform crits rarely flash; boss kills always do (with the anime look on).
- Accessory switching only rebuilds the merged outline because all 5 accessory meshes stay on the rig (hidden ones get a zero matrix). A hero body that adds more than 6 parts would hit MAX_RIG_PARTS (40).
- `tests/arenaCoop.test.ts` "client mirrors broken cover and falling pillars" failed once in 4 isolated runs and in one full `npm test` run; it passed in the others. It runs no render() (where all of this lane lives), so it looks like a pre-existing seed-dependent flake.
