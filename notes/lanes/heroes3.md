# Lane: heroes3 (Ronin and Tinker, recorded voices for the mechanic heroes)

## What players get

### Ronin (melee) — unlock: Point Blank (defeat 150 enemies within 2.5 m of you in one run)
A wandering swordsman in a wide straw kasa, red sash and headband tails, with a long katana out of the arm cannon. +30 life, +1 armor, 5% faster. The hero kit replaces the loadout's clicks (the LOADOUT rows say "RONIN USES KATANA / IAIDO DASH").
- **LEFT CLICK, Katana**: a 3-hit combo while fire is held. Hits 1 and 2 sweep a 130° arc in front (2.6 m reach), alternating sides; hit 3 is a heavy cleave (x2.4 damage, +0.6 m reach, x1.5 arc, x1.8 knockback, a 0.35 s stagger on non-bosses, hit-stop) and a slightly longer recovery. A pause of 0.75 s restarts the combo. Every swing cuts enemy bullets out of the air. Feel: a sweeping slash trail (revealed in 0.08 s, then fading), the arm sweeps across and the cleave twists the body, sparks along the blade, camera kick toward the aim, squash on every enemy hit, per-weapon hit-stop/crit-stop.
  - Tracks: Keen Edge (damage, reach, cleave), Flurry (swing speed, arc), **Sword Waves** (rank 1: the cleave throws a crescent wave that flies 8 m and cuts up to 8 enemies and enemy bullets; rank 3: every swing does) — the Ronin stays viable at range.
  - Evolutions: **Tempest Blade** (every swing throws a bigger, faster wave that flies twice as far; Projectile) and **Crimson Edge** (longer, wider, burning swings, the cleave goes all the way round; Fire, x1.25).
- **RIGHT CLICK, Iaido Dash**: hold to focus (0.6 s to full; focus builds while recharging too), release to dash along your aim (34 m/s, 3.6-8 m with focus) with i-frames. Everything the dash passes through takes a light cut (x0.5-1 damage) and is marked; 0.22 s after the dash every marked enemy is cut again (x1-3 damage) with a white slash line, hit-stop and shake. Enemy bullets on the path are cut. 1.6 s recharge.
  - Tracks: Deep Cut (damage), Long Step (distance, width), Calm Mind (focus time, recharge).
  - Evolutions: **Phantom Step** (the afterimage cuts the path again 0.45 s later at x0.6; each kill gives back 40% of the recharge) and **Raijin Strike** (the delayed cut chains lightning from everything it cuts; Shock, x1.15).
  - Right-click rules: Quick Draw/Capacitor speed the focus, `stats.chargeDmg` scales it, Overcharge lets focus go to 130% for a x1.3 cut, Split Shot (`chargeSplit`) adds afterimage cuts, Pacifist Core blocks it.
- **Counter-slash**: every landed parry (parry key, any source) answers with a 3.8 m slash all round (x3.5 the katana's damage, big knockback, "COUNTER!", four slash marks, hit-stop).
- **Ultimate THOUSAND CUTS** (3 s, untouchable): time slows (a 0.6 s beat of real slow motion solo, everything within 15 m crawls at 80% slow), a cut lands every 0.07 s round-robin over the 12 closest enemies within 11 m (2.2 x typical enemy life each, bosses x0.35), drawn as white/crimson slash marks, then the sheathe: one more 4x cut on everything that was cut, a crimson X on each.

### Tinker (summoner) — unlock: Workshop (hold 4 auto weapons at level 5 or higher in one run)
A young mechanic with big goggles, a tool backpack (its cog spins) and a wrench. +15% pickup range. Keeps the loadout's left click; RIGHT CLICK is the beacon.
- **Bots (passive)**: a little bot assembles at the Tinker's back every 4.5 s (x cooldown) up to the cap: 3 at team level 1, +1 every 5 levels, max 6. Bots trail the Tinker in formation, chase enemies within 9 m (a boss near the Tinker first; 13 m leash) and zap every 0.75 s within 4.5 m for 10 + 1 x team level (then the Tinker's damage stats; tag Chain; 1.3x faster on bosses and elites). They soak enemy bullets (3 each, then they pop). In co-op the team shares a budget of 24 bots (with 8 Tinkers, 3 each).
- **RIGHT CLICK, Turret Beacon**: press to drop a pylon at the cursor (2-9 m). For 10 s it zaps the nearest enemy in 7 m (a boss first) 2.5 times a second (18 damage, x chargeDmg; right-click hits Expose), its 3.5 m Repair Field heals heroes 4 life/s and makes the Tinker's bots zap 40% faster and repair. 12 s recharge (pressing again after it moves the beacon).
  - Tracks: Turret (damage, rate, range), Repair Field (heal, radius, bot boost), Battery (duration, recharge).
  - Evolutions: **Fortress Beacon** (two zaps per shot, +3 m range, Shock) and **Med Bay** (+1.5 m field, x2.5 repair, bots even faster).
- **Ultimate SCRAP LEGION** (1.2 s cast, untouchable): six big mechs drop in around the Tinker one after another (dust, shake), fight for 9 s (blast the nearest enemy in 9 m every 0.5 s: 1.4 x typical enemy life plus a 1.6 m splash, bosses x0.35), then pop. Fewer per Tinker in big parties: `max(2, round(6 x min(1, 3 / players)))` (4 players: 5 each, 8 players: 2 each).

### Voices
- Duelist, Furnace, Twin, Ronin and Tinker speak with their recorded barks (`RECORDED_VOICES`), captions in src/audio/voiceText.ts (from scripts/audio/sounds.mjs), speaker names for CAPTIONS.
- The synthesized voice stays the fallback: `speaksRecorded()` (src/audio/heroVoice.ts) lets the synth bark play for a recorded hero only when its recorded line for that moment is missing (not in the pack or not loaded yet). Moments without any recorded line (hurt, dash, parry, dodge, death) stay quiet for recorded heroes, as they already were for the Rookie/Bulwark/Dash/Ace, so a hero never mixes two voices. Synth profiles for the Ronin and Tinker exist for that fallback.
- Sounds: `swordSlash` / `swordCombo` / `swordDash` / `summon` / `minionZap` cues play the recorded sword_slash, sword_combo, sword_dash, summon and minion_zap samples (mix.ts `cueSamples` and `weaponSamples`) over a small synth layer (sfx.ts).

## Co-op
- Ronin: swings, waves and the dash-strike's cut line are weapon net state (`Katana.net` = seq/step/angle/reach/arc + waves, `Iaido.net` = focus/cut seq/path), so every machine draws every Ronin. The client dashes its own Ronin (`heroes3ClientStep` -> `Iaido.predict` -> `Player.lunge`) and sends the usual input; the host builds focus from the remote right click and, on release, sweeps the path the client's hero actually travels (0.2 s of slack for latency), cuts and marks there. Local heroes (host, local co-op) are dashed by the host (`Iaido.lunge` request, never for remote heroes). Teleports (jump pads, toys) drop the client's predicted focus.
- Tinker: bots are one pool on the host (fixed slots); the snapshot's `h3.m` carries [slot, owner|kind|zapping, x, z, zap x, zap z] per bot in the client's interest box, clients glide to it and draw zap beams. `h3.s` carries slash marks (Thousand Cuts, counter-slash) by sequence number; `h3.ck` this client's close kills (Point Blank is checked on each player's own machine). The beacon is weapon net state; repair is host-side and reaches clients as life.
- Hit-stop for the cleave, the cut and the counter goes through `heroHitStop` (systems/skill.ts): solo freezes, online holds only the hitter's picture (the `hs` skill event for remote heroes).
- `npm run coop` now plays a host Tinker and a client Ronin (`COOP_HEROES=rookie,rookie` for the old pair) and checks at 20 s that the bots exist on both machines and the client's sword hits on the host, and at 70 s that the client's dash-strike has cut something on the host.

## Performance
`npm run bench` (production build, headless SwiftShader, 15 s, on a machine at load average ~90, so absolute numbers are inflated): the default heavy scene measured 0.55 ms per sim step, 2.91 ms CPU per 60 fps frame; the same scene as a Tinker with 24 bots (the whole team budget) and a beacon measured `sim.weapons+specials` 0.219 ms vs 0.111 ms, while every other phase also ran 1.4-1.6x slower in that run (machine noise): the lane's own cost is about +0.05-0.1 ms per step for 24 bots (retarget every 12 steps via the spatial hash, bullet soak every other step, zaps through the normal damage pipeline). Bots, mechs and zap beams are three instanced meshes (upload only live slots); slash marks one more. The Ronin adds two small meshes per katana (trail, wave crescents instanced) and two per Iaido; their shaders compile at run start (kept "visible" with an empty draw range), not on the first swing.

## Unfinished / risky
- **Remote dash-strike timing** (integ2, after the netcode lane): the host used to decide a client's strike from its right-click release in the input stream and sweep the client's hero as its positions arrived (0.2 s slack). With the netcode lane's simulated link (latency, jitter, head-of-line resends) inputs arrive late and bunched, the host keeps only the newest, so it missed releases or swept after the window: `npm run coop` saw no dash-strike damage on the host. Now the client sends `iai` [focus, start, end] when its dash ends; the host validates it (recharge with the usual 6% slack, focus, path length, start near the hero) and cuts along that exact path against enemies rewound to the client's view (`lagSteps`/`rewindEnemy`, like remote bullets), with the delayed cut shortened by the lag. A connected client's release no longer starts a strike on the host (`Iaido.clientDriven`); autopilot heroes keep the old release path. A client may still see a dash the host refused (recharge mismatch): it dashes without cutting.
- **Bot targeting cost**: each bot retargets every 12 steps via the spatial hash and checks enemy bullets every other step (64 bots x 500 bullets worst case, about 0.05 ms). Fine in the bench; watch it if the bot budget grows.
- **Hero-only clicks and other lanes**: anything that builds loadouts from `settings.loadout` alone (e.g. local co-op slot labels, the coop2 help-ghost `kit`) still shows the loadout's clicks for a Ronin/Tinker; the run itself uses the kit. The PvP rebuild uses `startRun`, so it gets the kit.
- **iconArt.ts is generated**: if another lane also added icons, re-run `npm run icons` after merging scripts/icons.mjs instead of merging iconArt.ts by hand.
- Balance is bot-measured only (god mode); the bot doesn't play melee like a person (it kites and swings at whatever is in front). The Tinker's boss times vary most with its auto weapons.
- `npm run coop` on this machine ran under a load average of ~100 (twelve parallel builders): page loads took 1-4 minutes and several older scenarios (turret, jump pad, team move, pillar) failed on timing in some runs, with and without the new heroes; see Verification.
