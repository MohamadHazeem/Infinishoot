# Lane: heroes4 (Medic and Brawler)

## What players get

### Medic (healer) — unlock: Lifesaver (clear a stage after dropping below 20% life in it)
A field nurse with a white field cap (a red cross on the front and one on top, so it reads from the top-down camera), a head mirror, a white med pack and a green lens on the arm cannon. +0.4 life/s, +50% revive speed (the existing `reviveSpeed` stat). The kit replaces both clicks (`HeroDef.clicks`, the LOADOUT rows say "MEDIC USES MENDING BEAM / REVIVE DRONE").
- **LEFT CLICK, Mending Beam**: a 12 m beam while fire is held, ticking 10 times a second (16 damage per tick at rank 0). Each tick burns through the first 3 enemies on it (closest first); bosses and mini-bosses on the beam always take the tick and never use up that budget, and take x1.75 (the Medic's right click deals no damage, so it never Exposes). Every tick heals each teammate the beam passes through (1.4 life) and mends the Medic by 5% of the damage dealt (at most 8 life/s). The beam also hits barrels.
  - Tracks: Intensity (+3/+3/+3 and faster ticks/+4 damage), Wide Lens (range, width, +1/+2 pierce), Bedside Manner (teammate heal, mend share).
  - Evolutions: **Surge Beam** (+2 pierce, every 6th tick chains lightning 2 hops from the last enemy hit; Shock, x1.3) and **Lifeline** (wider, heals and mends x2, chills what it touches; Frost).
- **RIGHT CLICK, Revive Drone**: press to launch. If a teammate is down within 16 m of the Medic, the drone flies (26 m/s) to the one closest to the cursor, hovers 1.2 s with a tether and a closing ring, then picks them up at 40% life (+10 life patch, 2 s untouchable): "DRONE REVIVE!". 20 s recharge. With nobody down it shields the living teammate closest to the cursor (within 4 m of it), else the Medic: the drone orbits them inside a bubble, and the next 2 hits within 6 s are blocked outright ("BLOCKED", 0.5 s grace). 9 s recharge. Right-click rules: Quick Draw / Capacitor speed the recharge, Pacifist Core blocks it.
  - Tracks: Rapid Response (recharge, revive speed and range), Nanoshield (+hits, longer shields), Patch Kit (heal on arrival, revive life).
  - Evolutions: **Guardian Drone** (+1 hit; every blocked hit zaps the attacker for 3 typical enemy lives and its neighbours within 3 m for half; Shock) and **Phoenix Drone** (revives at full life, 3 s untouchable, a healing fire burst: 30% max life to every hero within 6 m and 2 typical lives of Fire damage to enemies there).
- **Passive, regeneration aura**: teammates within 6 m of the Medic regenerate 2 life/s (never the Medic itself). A dashed mint ring shows the range in co-op only.
- **Ultimate FIELD HOSPITAL** (6 s, not untouchable): a 7 m ward opens where the Medic stands (a mint floor, a turning white ring, two floating white crosses, rising sparkles). Inside: allies heal 10% max life per second; enemy shots fizzle; **nobody inside can drop below 1 life** ("SAVED!") while it is open and for 1 s after; a downed teammate inside gets up after 1.5 s at 35% life ("BACK ON YOUR FEET!"); every 0.5 s a pulse deals 1 typical enemy life to every enemy inside (bosses x0.35) and slows them 40%.

### Brawler (melee grappler) — unlock: Wrecking Crew (20 environmental kills in one run)
A wrestling champion with big red boxing gloves, a yellow headband with tails, a spiky dark crest and a gold champion belt. +50 life, +3 armor, +25% knockback, 5% slower. Both clicks are the kit.
- **LEFT CLICK, Iron Fists**: a 3-punch combo while fire is held (4 punches/s, 30 damage, 2 m reach, 95° arc): jab and cross alternate fists, the third is a **haymaker** (x2.6, +0.4 m reach, wider, a 0.45 s stagger on non-bosses, hit-stop, a ground shock ring) that **launches** enemies along the punch at up to 34 m/s (x3 the punch knockback, then the Brawler's knockback stat). A launch is fast enough to count as "knocked into" lava (instant kill), thorns (skewer), quicksand and ice (slide and splat on a wall), so the arena does the finishing: those are environmental kills (score bonus, the ENV KILL popups, the objective). Elites fly half as far, bosses don't fly. Punches swat enemy bullets out of the air.
  - Tracks: Iron Knuckles (damage, heavier haymaker), Speed Bag (rate, arc, reach), Haymaker (launch distance; rank 2 a 2.2 m shockwave round the target, rank 4 bigger).
  - Evolutions: **Thunder Fists** (every punch Shocks, the haymaker chains lightning 3 hops; x1.1) and **Meteor Fist** (the haymaker is a burning ground slam; Explosive, Fire; x1.3).
- **RIGHT CLICK, Grapple**: press to grab the closest liftable enemy in front (2.4 m, a 130° cone). Hold to carry it in front of you (up to 1.6 s), release to throw it along your aim (24 m/s for 0.5 s, ~10 m). The thrown enemy smashes everything it touches on the way (40 damage x chargeDmg, knocked away at 16 m/s, "STRIKE!" on the third), pops barrels it passes, and lands for the same damage plus a 1.1 m splash. If it slams into a wall or cover it takes x1.5, the cover takes 60 damage ("SLAM!"): crates break and **pillars topple** onto whatever is behind them. A thrown enemy flies with hazard-speed knockback, so throwing it into lava or a chasm kills it outright. Bosses, mini-bosses, the Reaper, PvP stand-ins and burrowed sharks can't be lifted: they get a heavy **uppercut** (x5 damage, a short stagger, a rising white crescent). 1.3 s recharge (x1.4 after an uppercut); whiffs retry after 0.3 s. Right-click rules: recharge from Quick Draw / Capacitor, damage x `stats.chargeDmg`, right-click hits Expose, Pacifist Core blocks it.
  - Tracks: Body Slam (damage, splash, heavier uppercut), Strong Arm (throw distance and speed, grab reach), Quick Grip (recharge, carry time).
  - Evolutions: **Piledriver** (thrown enemies explode on landing, 3 m, launching everything around and breaking cover; Explosive, x1.25) and **Giant Swing** (hold to spin the grabbed enemy round you at 2.4 m as a club that hits everything it touches every 0.4 s, then let go to hurl it).
- **Dash: shoulder charge**: every dash (normal dash rules, i-frames, Dash Cells) bowls over enemies within 1.35 m (22 damage, then the Brawler's stats; knocked forward and aside at 22 m/s, so a charge also sends enemies into hazards) and shoulders through crates and pillars on its way (30 damage to cover). Damage source "Shoulder Charge" (`shoulder`).
- **Ultimate WRECKING BALL** (4 s, untouchable, x1.7 speed): a ground pound (6 m, 3 typical lives, launches), then the Brawler spins (the body turns, arms out): everything within 2.6 m takes 3.5 typical lives every 0.35 s and is launched along the spin at 32 m/s like bowling pins; enemies within 7 m drift into the spin; crates, pillars and barrels in the way break. Bosses take x0.35 and don't fly.

"-": the run hadn't reached that boss by 1100 s. heroes3's band for comparison: Rookie medians 17 / 37 / 19, Ronin 24 / 38 / 30. Medians now: Medic 23.5 / 41.5 / 91, Brawler 43.5 / 39. Both sit at or just past the slow end of the band, as their kits trade damage for something else: the Medic heals and revives (its right click deals no damage, so it never Exposes; mortal bot runs show the sustain), the Brawler's damage needs it in melee and the bot only closes in on bosses (it kites the swarm, so it levels slower: 17-19k kills a run vs 29-37k for the Rookie). Both are slow on the flying Hive Queen, like the Ronin.

Mortal bot runs (700 s, Normal): the Medic reached stage 3 and was still alive at the end in 4 of 4 (seeds 3-4 at the final numbers, 1-2 one step earlier; ending at 100-140 life: the beam's self-mend carries it). The Brawler (final numbers, seeds 1-4) beat stage 1's King in 4 of 4 and reached stage 3 in 2 of 4 (died to Rex twice and to a Veteran Rhino in stage 3; one survived the 700 s). The Rookie reached stage 3 in 2 of 4. A first melee autopilot that backed off 7 m let the Brawler bot die to King Bopper's shockwave rings or the boss duel's ring of fire in 4 of 4 (it backed out of the ring).

Changes made from these runs: beam 9 -> 16 damage per tick, range 10 -> 12, Intensity ranks +2/+2/+2/+3 -> +3/+3/+3/+4; bosses and mini-bosses on the beam always take the tick (outside the pierce budget) and take x1.75 (`heroes4Tuning.medic.beamBoss`); fists 24 x 3.6/s -> 30 x 4/s; grab 34 -> 40, uppercut x4 -> x6.5 with a x1.2 (was x1.4) recharge; Brawler +40 life/+2 armor -> +50/+3 and a little life per kill (`killHeal` 0.05, a third of one Vampire Fang, through the shared lifesteal budget). The autopilot (`pilotHeroes4`) plays the Brawler hit-and-run against bosses (1.8 m past the boss's radius while it walks or recovers, 6 m while it winds up or attacks) and never backs out of a boss duel's ring of fire.

## Co-op
- Host-side: the beam's teammate heals, the regeneration aura, the drone (launch choice, flight, revive, shield orbit), the shields and the 1-life floor (`heroes4HitCare`, called from `hurtHero` after the Glass rule and before the Frost Totem), thrown enemies, the shoulder charge (uses the remote hero's synced `dashing` and velocity) and Field Hospital.
- Clients: the beam (`MedBeam.net` = on, angle, length, width), the drone (`RevDrone.net` = mode, position, bubble centre, revive progress, shield hits), punches (`Fists.net` = seq, step, angle, reach, arc), the grab (`Grab.net` = holding, swing angle, uppercut/throw seqs) are weapon net state; held and thrown enemies are ordinary enemy records; Field Hospital wards and this client's environmental-kill count ride the snapshot's `h4` field (`{ z: [owner, x, z, t, dur]..., ek }`, undefined when there is nothing to say). Wrecking Ball's speed on a client's own Brawler comes from the synced ultimate timer (systems/skill.ts `applySkillNet`), the spin is drawn from it on every machine.
- Lifesaver is checked on each player's own machine (its own life and the synced mode); Wrecking Crew counts on the host (`heroes4OnKill`, every `ENV_SOURCES` kill credited to that hero) and reaches clients in `h4.ek`.
- `npm run coop` keeps its default pair (Tinker, Ronin); `COOP_HEROES=medic,brawler npm run coop` plays a host Medic and a client Brawler and checks (24 s) the host's beam damage, the client's punches landing on the host and the host's drone on the client, (74 s) the client's grabs/throws/charges landing on the host. Result on this machine: `COOP_HEROES=medic,brawler npm run coop` PASSED (host beam damage 4100, the client Brawler's fists 139 and grabs/charges 56 on the host, the client saw the host's drone next to the Medic; snapshots avg 1425 B, max 10.9 KB); the default `npm run coop` (Tinker, Ronin) PASSED too.

## Unfinished / risky
- **No recorded voices**: both heroes speak only with the synthesized voice; no ElevenLabs lines were generated (no key or network on the build machine). Banter lines (`BANTER_HEROES` in coopplay.ts) don't include them, so they stay quiet in co-op banter.
- **Remote grab feel**: the grab and throw are host-judged from the client's right click like every right-click weapon; on a laggy link the held enemy trails the client's own hero by the round trip (it is placed in front of where the host has the hero). A client-side prediction of the held enemy was not added.
- **Thrown-enemy wall detection** compares the enemy's travel with its expected travel after the enemy update; an enemy that is pushed back by the crowd separation in the same step can read as a crash a little early. Cosmetic in practice (it still lands with damage).
- **Field Hospital floor** applies to any hero inside any open ward (including other Medics' wards); it doesn't stop one-shot rules that bypass `hurtHero` (none found), and Glass mode (`meta.rule('glass')`) still leaves 1 life inside a ward on purpose (the ward is the point of the ultimate).
- The Medic's damage is lower than the ranged heroes against the flying Hive Queen (she hovers out of the 12 m beam); the Brawler leans on its ultimate and the bot doesn't play melee like a person (it kites the swarm and only closes in on bosses).
- Tips: no first-time tip was added for the two heroes (the tips2 lane owns TIP2_IDS); the hero cards and the LOADOUT note describe the kits.
