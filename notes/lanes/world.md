# Lane: world (new biomes, loop remixes, boss arenas, title attract mode)

> **Merge note (v1.6.0 merge):** Sky Ruins was dropped in favour of main's Sky Fortress (near-identical idea); its wind lanes, islets, blossom trees and textures are gone. Loop 2 is now Neon City, Toy Factory, Sky Fortress, Ashen Magma (PLAN.md decision log). The arena builder is main's, with the factory pieces ported in.

## What players get
- **Toy Factory** (stage 5, 11, ...): foam play-mat floors in soft pastels, riveted walkways, building-block ruins with studs, ring-stacker / wind-up robot / spinning-top "trees", alphabet blocks, a gumball edge, a ball-pit sea that drifts like a conveyor, big gears half-sunk in it, confetti in the air and five conveyor belts. Hazard: **crusher stamps**, a toy press over a hazard-striped pad that winds down (red danger zone with a closing ring) and slams every 5.5 s: enemies on the pad are flattened (environmental kill, counts for the env-kill objective), heroes on it take a hit (dash or jump to dodge).
- **Sky Ruins** (stage 6, 12, ...): a floating island over a sea of clouds far below, with rocky roots under its cliffs, cloud puffs round the edge, small islets bobbing in the distance, mint sky-grass with clover, white-and-gold marble ruins, broken columns and blossom trees, wind streaks blowing past. Hazard: **wind lanes**, wide strips of scrolling wind streaks that push heroes and enemies along (3 m/s).
- **Loop remixes**: a classic biome that comes back in loop 2+ gets a new look (gameplay and hazards unchanged): **Aurora Peaks** (teal snow, violet sky light, glowing crystal clusters, aurora glimmers), **Ashen Magma** (grey ash floor, basalt columns, falling ash, a smoky sunset), **Autumn Meadow** (golden grass with fallen leaves, orange canopies, pumpkins, falling leaves, golden tufts), **Night Dunes** (always night, lilac sand, glowing crystals, fireflies).
- **Biome order**: loop 1 is unchanged (meadow, dunes, peaks, magma). Loop 2: Toy Factory, Sky Ruins, Aurora Peaks, Ashen Magma. Loop 3: Autumn Meadow, Night Dunes, Toy Factory, Sky Ruins. Later loops alternate rows 2 and 3. The final boss still ends every 4th stage.
- **Boss arenas**: when a boss lands, the ground around it is dressed for that boss and cleared ~1.5 s after it dies: King Bopper gets a red carpet ring with gold borders, a crown emblem and banner posts; Rampage Rex glowing cracks radiating from his landing, scattered bones and a half-buried rib cage; Hive Queen a honeycomb floor with honey and wax lumps; Omega a tech octagon with grid lines, turning glowing rings and pylons. Everything rises out of / grows across the ground, nothing is solid (escape routes stay open), props skip spots inside walls.
- **Title attract mode**: two seconds after the title shows, a live demo plays behind the logo: the autoplay brain with a random unlocked loadout and three auto weapons, in a random biome the player has already reached (a fresh save sees the meadow), framed to the right of the menu. Gameplay sounds, damage numbers and shakes are off; music stays the title theme. It restarts on death or every 60 s in a new place and freezes the moment a menu opens; starting a run rebuilds everything.
- Two new music styles (data entries only): "Toy Factory" (bright mixolydian four-on-the-floor) and "Sky Ruins" (airy lydian half-time). They unlock in the jukebox on reaching stages 5 / 6.

## Where things are
- `src/data/biomes.ts`: `BiomeDef` now carries everything per biome as data (weather, rain chance, tufts, shore, lilies, glints, dust and print tints, footstep surface, ambience bed, music track, hazard, wind lanes, belt count, night/dusk, grade, decor, wall/tree/rock/edge styles, tints) plus `remixes`. `BIOME_ORDER` + `biomeKeyForStage(stage)` / `biomeForStage(stage)` / `biomeByKey('meadow:autumn')`. `biomeForce.key` pins a biome (test links / debug only, solo).
- `src/render/biomeTextures.ts`: the new pixel textures. `src/world/biomeProps.ts`: block walls, toy trees, blossoms, rock styles (blocks, columns, basalt, pumpkins, crystals), edge shapes, floating-island underside, animated decor (`ArenaAnim`). `src/world/fieldArena.ts`: wires them in (new rng use only in new-biome branches, so classic layouts are unchanged for a seed), crusher pads, wind lanes (arena conveyors with `kind: 'wind'` and their own `speed`), `arena.anim`.
- `src/game/hazards.ts`: `crusherTuning`, `crusherPhase` / `crusherSlammed` / `pressHeight` (pure), `windTuning`; `biomeHazard` reads the biome data. `src/game/systems/worldHazards.ts`: host crusher telegraph, slam effects and damage (called from `updateHazards`).
- `src/world/worldRender.ts`: per-frame local presentation (called from the worldFx system): decor animation, crusher presses (host: director time; clients: a local clock pulled toward the synced stage timer), boss dressing lifecycle. `src/world/bossDressing.ts`: the four dressings.
- `src/render/biomeLook.ts`: grades for the new biomes, remix grade overrides, `dusk` floor for twilight remixes, `isNightStage` honours the biome's `night`.
- `src/game/autopilot.ts`: the autoplay brain, factored out of `src/dev/bot.ts` (the dev bot now maps its intent to keys/mouse exactly as before). `src/game/systems/attract.ts`: the title demo (`attractTuning`), installed on Game as `attractTick` and called from `Game.update` on the title.
- `src/world/worldDebug.ts`: debug panel folder "World: biomes & title demo".

## Knobs
- `crusherTuning` (period 5.5 s, warn 1.3 s, radius 1.8 m, heroDmg 16 x stage damage, eliteFrac 0.3, count 5), `windTuning` (speed 3 m/s, width 4 m), `bossDressingTuning` (radius 11 m, rise 1.2 s, sink 1.6 s, linger 1.5 s), `attractTuning` (enabled, delay 2 s, length 60 s, deadWait 1.6 s, startTime 90 s, crowd 0.7, autoWeapons 3 at levels 2-4, frameX 7 m). All in the debug panel, plus a biome picker ("Biome (next stage)" + "Reload this stage").
- Per-biome look: `src/data/biomes.ts` (remix entries override any look field).

## Unfinished / risky
- The new biomes have no biome-native enemies yet (the CONTENT lane keys theirs by biome id: `'factory'`, `'skyruins'`; get the current one with `biomeForStage(game.arenaStage).id`).
- Sound: `biomeAudio()` / `groundAudio()` / `biomeTrack()` in src/audio are no longer used by the game (it reads `ambience`, `footstep` and `music` from the biome data), but their tests still pass; the sound lane may want to delete or redirect them. The factory reuses the magma bed and room, the sky ruins the peaks bed. Tests: `tests/music.test.ts` now expects 14 tracks.
- The title demo rebuilds the arena when it (re)starts: a one-off hitch of roughly one arena build (see above) every 60 s on the title, not during menus or runs.
- Crusher presses on co-op clients follow the synced stage timer (20 Hz) and run on a local clock during the boss (the timer is hidden then), so a press can be up to a frame or two off the host; damage and slam effects are host-side and arrive on the effects bus, so gameplay is unaffected.
- Wind lane speed is baked into the lane when the arena is built (the debug slider affects the next stage).
- Hero footsteps on a belt are 'metal' except in biomes without belts (wind lanes keep the biome's footstep).
- Not verified: real GPUs (only SwiftShader), `npm run coop` (needs the PeerJS broker), touch devices with the title demo running, the Electron build.

## PLAN.md decision-log paragraph
- 2026-10-19: World lane (notes/lanes/world.md). Two new biomes and loop remixes: stages now cycle `BIOME_ORDER` instead of `(stage - 1) mod 4`: loop 1 is unchanged; loop 2 is Toy Factory (play-mat floors, building-block ruins, wind-up toys, a ball-pit sea with gears, crusher stamps that slam every 5.5 s) and Sky Ruins (a floating island over clouds, marble ruins, wind lanes that push at 3 m/s), then Aurora Peaks and Ashen Magma; loop 3 is Autumn Meadow, Night Dunes (always night), Toy Factory and Sky Ruins; later loops alternate. Remixes are data on the biome (look only; hazards and gameplay stay the biome's). Everything biome-specific is now data on `BiomeDef` (weather, tufts, shore, dust, footsteps, ambience bed, music, hazard, night/dusk, grade), so new biomes need no switch statements; clients still derive the biome from the stage number. Crushers run on the host on the director clock (clients pose the presses from the synced timer; slam effects and damage arrive as usual); wind lanes are arena conveyors with their own speed. Bosses get cosmetic arena dressing built locally from the boss id (King carpet ring, Rex cracks and bones, Queen honeycomb, Omega tech platform), never solid, cleaned up after the fight. The title screen plays a live attract demo: the dev bot's decisions moved to src/game/autopilot.ts (shared, no cheats in production), the demo runs the real simulation in a reached biome with gameplay sounds, damage numbers and shakes muted and the profile locked (`saveLock`), restarts on death or every 60 s and freezes the moment a menu opens. Music: two data-only track styles (Toy Factory, Sky Ruins), jukebox now 14 tracks.
