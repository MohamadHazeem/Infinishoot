# Lane: coopstress (four-player online co-op under stress)

## How it is tested
- **`npm run coop4`** (scripts/coop-stress.mjs): 1 host + 3 clients, each its own headless Chromium context (own localStorage, own stable player id), against a Vite dev server (HMR off, so edits elsewhere can't reload the pages mid-run). Signalling through PeerJS's public broker, like the shipped game; the data channels are page to page. Heroes Rookie / Bulwark / Ace / Dash (four roles), revive pool on, JOIN ANYTIME on. Every page plays with the dev bot (`botAuto`).
  - Client 2 runs with `--lag` ms (default 70) of extra delay on every message in and out (an in-page wrapper around its `NetClient`, order kept).
  - Scenario (host simulated seconds): enemy mix and a shielded elite around the clients (6), emotes + pings from every client (12), a mech suit for client 1 and a supply crate by client 2 (18), the flood world event (24), a team move from client 1 with all four together (30), a map chest (36), **client 3 drops** (its peer destroyed, no `bye`: host autopilot + AUTOPILOT tag on the other clients) (42) and **drops back in** with the same player id (52), client 2 downed and revived (62), **revive pool**: client 1 downed, waits out the BUY BACK delay, sends `cobuy` (68), **team ultimate**: everyone's ultimate filled at once, the bots fire (76), the stage boss (86), phase 3 (100), the boss killed with its finisher and **one owned chest per hero** that must reach its owner (110), then level-ups, the (secret) shop with its relic screen, the route vote, the portal and 40 s of stage 2. After the co-op run: **Versus Swarm** (re-hosted, client 1 is the Overlord and orders a Swarm Pack through `vcmd`; the host benches its hero) and **local co-op** (a fresh page, `?test&localcoop=run&lcpads=3&god`, 4 heroes for 20 s).
  - Every 4 sim seconds, for every client: stage, seed, mode, team level, kills, hero count, boss id and phase, mini-boss, each hero's left/dead flags, HP, build keys, mech state and position; the client's own gold, passives and Mods; **stuck screens** (a client on a level-up / relic / shop / wait / route screen the host has closed; the host waiting on a pick a client never sees; the host in a menu for 4 checks; the route map open for 3). A difference that lasts two checks fails the run; any page error or console error fails it. A client whose last snapshot is more than 1.5 sim s behind the host is skipped for that check (and fails after 5 such checks in a row).
  - Short-lived things (emote bubbles, team-ultimate camera beats, autopilot tags, world events, mech suits) are latched by a recorder that runs after every game step on each page, so a late check still sees them.
  - **Everything is scheduled in the host's simulated seconds, not wall time.** This machine ran 10+ other lanes' browsers and tests at the same time (load average 65-145 on 18 cores); the pages simulated at 0.03x-0.9x real time. Wall-clock waits made every timing check meaningless, so waits are `simPause()` / `until()` in sim seconds with wall caps.
  - Reports: snapshot wire bytes per client (PeerJS binarypack, the measuring taken out of the update time), the host's update time per step (avg / p50 / p95 / p99, share over 16.7 ms, the slowest steps), sim speed, how far behind each client's snapshots fell.
- **tests/coopstress.test.ts** (part of `npm test`, ~20 s): the same four-player flow in one process (host + 3 client `Game`s, messages passed by hand like coop2Coop.test.ts), deterministic: lobby hellos with player ids, a 5th player refused, the starting shop, a double level-up for everyone, the boss and its owned chests, the secret merchant's relic screen, the shop and the route vote, the portal, stage 2, a drop during a level-up (autopilot picks), a split route vote with an empty seat, the drop-in with the same player id, and after each phase: every client agrees with the host (mode, stage, level, heroes, builds, own gold) and no client screen is stuck. It prints the host's cost with 4 players and ~300 enemies.

## Bugs found and fixed
1. **Boss chests could be stranded (4 players).** `Pickups.update` picked the *nearest* living hero and then asked `canTake`; if that hero wasn't the owner, nobody could take it, even its owner standing next to it, as long as a teammate was closer. Chests only open by walking over them, and the stage-clear vacuum pulled gems only (the comment said chests too). With four heroes bunched up at a boss, the in-process test left all four chests on the ground into the shop and route; the first teammate through the portal then threw them away with the stage. Fix (src/game/pickups.ts, spice.ts): the nearest hero *that may take it* collects it (gears at the cap and teammates' chests are skipped, not blocking), and the stage clear sets `vacuumChests`, so every chest flies to whoever may open it (boss chests to their owner) during the clear. Map chest pickups from elites also come in at the clear now (solo too).
2. **Lobby players could never reclaim their hero after a drop (coop2).** Reclaiming matches the stable player id (`pid`) against `coop2State.pids`, but only mid-run joiners had theirs recorded; everyone who came in through the lobby had none. A dropped lobby player's `hello` therefore counted as a new player: refused with "That run is full (4 players)" in a 4-player run (their own hero still held the seat on autopilot), or given a *second* hero next to the autopiloted one with 2-3 players. The netcode lane's new stale-connection drop (`netcodeHello`) relies on the same `pids`. Fix: the lobby `hello`'s pid is kept in the `LobbyEntry` (`pid?`), `newRun` fills `pids` from the lobby; with no pid on record the name is used, as for old clients (src/game/game.ts, netHost.ts, systems/coop2.ts).
3. **A join that timed out kept a zombie connection.** If `client.ready` timed out (12 s, or ICE "failed"), `joinGame` gave up but never closed the `NetClient`; when that connection opened later, it held one of the host's slots and its messages (`barrel`, `chestspawn`, `lv`, `go`...) were applied to the client's *title screen* game. Seen on the loaded machine (a rejoin took 4 tries). Fix (systems/online.ts): close the client on failure, and only the current `NetClient` may feed `onClientMessage`. The stress script retries a failed join like a player would and fails the run if any host message reaches the page after a given-up try.
4. **Route vote waited out the timer for an empty seat.** A dropped player's autopilot closed its menus and agreed to rewind votes, but never voted on the route map, so a split vote (2 vs 1 of 4) had no majority until the 10 s timer ran out. Fix: `routeAutoVote` (systems/coop2.ts): once everyone at the keyboard has voted, the autopilot goes with the most-voted road (ties: the host's). The in-process test's split vote finishes in 0.7 s (10.5 s without the fix).
5. **5th player in the lobby.** The game had no lobby cap of its own (only the transport's 3 connections); with the netcode lane's spare reconnect slot (src/net/peer.ts) a 5th player could enter the lobby. Capped in the `hello` handler with the same one-line change the netcode lane made, so the two merge cleanly.

Test-side fixes (no game change): the dev bot now takes the secret merchant's relic on a client (it waited on the RELIC screen forever, which held the whole team in the shop); `npm run coop`'s team-move check expected the meter at exactly 0 after the move, but it starts charging again at once while the heroes stand together (now < 0.5).

## Numbers
Snapshot bytes are the real wire size (PeerJS binarypack of the message). Three clients, so the host sends three snapshots per tick.

| | `npm run coop4` (browsers, stage 1-2, run 5) | tests/coopstress.test.ts (one process, jsdom) |
|---|---|---|
| snapshots per client | 2,326-2,568 per client | 1,200 steps |
| average snapshot | 2,513-2,515 B per client (~49 KB/s per client at 20 Hz, ~147 KB/s upload for the host) | 3,215-3,231 B with ~300 enemies alive |
| largest snapshot | 7,937-8,196 B, none over 16 KB (no PeerJS chunking) | |
| `buildSnapshot` | 0.17-0.20 ms per client | 0.82-1.07 ms per client |
| host update per 1/60 s step (4 players, no rendering) | p50 0.30 ms, p95 1.3 ms, p99 2.2 ms; 0.09% of steps over 16.7 ms | 3.1-5.1 ms average (jsdom DOM shims included) |
| client update per step | 0.02-0.04 ms average (they mirror; rendering not counted) | |

- 2-player `npm run coop` for comparison (main before this lane): ~1,700 B average, ~11.5 KB max per snapshot, so a 4-player client's snapshot is ~1.5x a 2-player one (three more heroes in the body and more enemies near a spread-out team), and the host's upload is ~4.4x.
- **Sim speed**: the browser runs got 0.03x-0.9x real time (median ~0.3x) because the machine was shared with 10+ other lanes' browsers and tests (load average 65-145 on 18 cores). The per-step averages above exclude those stalls by using percentiles; every step over 0.5 s on the host was the frame a level-up or shop screen opened (DOM build and layout of the menu, up to 56-65 s under that load). Measured alone on the same machine, opening a level-up screen took 330-1,300 ms of main thread (mostly native style/layout work, `showLevelUp` forcing layout through `offsetWidth`); not co-op specific, noted for the HUD/perf lanes.
- Clients' snapshots fell at most 0.3-1.2 sim s behind the host (the 70 ms-lagged client the most).

## Checked and found in sync (no change needed)
- Level-ups for four (double level-up, WAIT screens, `go` closing everything), the starting shop, the post-boss shop, the secret merchant's relic screen, the route vote and portal into stage 2, all with one lagged client.
- Emotes and pings from three clients at once (every machine shows all three bubbles), mech suit state (`gp`) and supply crates (`gpw`), the flood world event, the team move with four heroes, map chests (`chestspawn`), down / revive, the revive-pool buy-back (`cobuy`, gold split, a pool life spent), the team ultimate (fired on the host, the camera beat on every client), the boss with its entrance, phases and finisher, owned chests, autopilot tags (`co.ai`) on the other clients while a player is away.
- Versus Swarm with a client Overlord (bench hero, `vcmd` orders accepted, swarmers spawned, every client mirrors the Overlord index) and local co-op with three fake pads (4 heroes fighting, no errors).

## Not fixed / notes for other lanes
- **Snapshot encoding scales with players** (src/net/snapCodec.ts, netcode lane's area): `SnapEncoder.encode` re-encodes the whole body (heroes, team, boss, arena...) for every client and then compares bytes; with 3 clients that is ~0.9-1.0 ms per client per snapshot (node/jsdom, ~300 enemies), most of it `writeSticky` and value encoding of fields that are identical for every client. Caching the encoded bytes of client-independent fields once per tick would cut the host's snapshot cost by about two thirds at 4 players. Not changed here (the codec is being reworked by the netcode lane).
- **Join timeout under load**: the 12 s connect timeout in `NetClient` (src/net/peer.ts) is often too short when the machine is starved (see above); real players on one PC per player won't see that, but a slow phone or a busy laptop might. The netcode lane owns the transport.
- A boss can still be killed before every hero gets near it: owned chests now fly in at the clear, but only to a living owner (a downed one is stood up by the clear first, so in practice always).
- The emote / ping rate limits, help ghosts (off in this run) and PvP were not stressed with four players.
- Real networks, real latency spread and packet loss: only one client had a fixed added delay; the netcode lane's simulator covers loss and jitter.
