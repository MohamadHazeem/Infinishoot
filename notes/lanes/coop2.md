# Lane: coop2 (drop-in co-op, revive pool, roles, team ultimate, emotes, help ghosts)

## What players get
- **Drop-in, drop-out co-op.** The host's room stays open during the run (JOIN ANYTIME, on by default; host lobby switch). A friend who types the code into PLAY ONLINE > JOIN while the run is going sees "The run is already going: you jump in at the next safe moment...", then:
  - joins at the next safe moment (fighting, cleared or portal; never on a level-up, shop, route screen or stage transition; given up after 90 s),
  - gets a hero built to a fair power level: their loadout, plus 85% of the team's level-ups picked automatically (loadout evolutions and ranks first, then weapons, items, Overdrive, filler last) and 60% of the team's average gold,
  - spawns 2.2 m from a living teammate on open ground with 3 s of spawn protection and a ring/burst, "LATE JOINED! · 3 PLAYERS · ENEMIES SCALE UP",
  - gets a full world keyframe (new snapshot encoder) plus the one-off state they missed: chests (and which are open), blown barrels, broken cover.
  - Enemy scaling (director `players`) follows the players in the run, joining or leaving.
- **Leaving.** Quitting on purpose (QUIT / leaving the run) sends `bye`: the hero leaves at once and cleanly. A dropped connection keeps the hero in the fight on **autopilot for 45 s** (the shared autopilot brain: kites, shoots, uses the special and the ultimate; auto-picks level-ups, closes its shop, agrees to TEAM REWIND votes), with an "AUTOPILOT 32S" tag over it. Then it leaves ("DIDN'T COME BACK").
- **Coming back.** Rejoining with the same code reclaims the hero: on autopilot it is simply handed back; after it left, it is rebuilt from the kept build (weapons, tracks, special, passives, Mods, relics, gold, gears). Players are matched by a stable id kept in localStorage (`infinishoot.coop.pid`), or by name for older clients. A fifth player is refused ("That run is full (4 players)").
- **HUD team panel** (right, just above the minimap and under the style meter; co-op and hosts only, never PvP): "JOINABLE · K7P2Q · 3/4", each hero's role chip in their colour, the team bonus ("TEAM x1.5: +1 ARMOR · +8% DAMAGE" or "MIX ROLES FOR A TEAM BONUS"), and TEAM LIVES hearts when the pool is on.
- **Shared revive pool** (host lobby: REVIVE POOL OFF / SHARED LIVES; off by default). The team shares 3 lives (+1 per stage clear, max 6). A downed hero that nobody is reviving can buy back in after 5 s: the BUY BACK panel (right of YOU'RE DOWN) says "[T] BUY BACK IN · 18●" and fills while you wait; press the team-move key (T / D-pad down). Cost: 12 + 6 x stage gold, paid by the whole team (split rule below). If the last hero falls and the pool has a life the team can pay for, it's spent automatically: "SAVED BY THE POOL!".
  - **Split rule** (`splitCost`, src/game/coop2.ts): everyone in the run (the downed hero too) pays an equal share; anyone who can't cover theirs pays all they have and the rest is split evenly among the others, repeated; whole gold only, odd coins go to whoever holds the most. If the whole team together can't pay, there's no buy-back. Each payer sees "-6●" over their hero.
- **Co-op roles** (src/data/coopRoles.ts): Bulwark and Furnace are TANK, Rookie is MEDIC, Ace and Duelist are DAMAGE, Dash and Twin are SUPPORT. Each distinct role in the party gives everyone its gift: tank +1 armor, medic +0.6 life/s, damage +8% damage, support +8% speed and +15% pickup range; x1 with 2 roles, x1.5 with 3, x2 with all 4. One role (e.g. four Rookies) gives nothing. Host-applied to live stats every step (`coopRoleStats` after `refreshLive`); speed and pickup range reach clients through the synced stats. Shown in the lobby (ROLES row with names and "3 ROLES: TEAM BONUS FOR EVERYONE") and the HUD panel.
- **Team ultimate.** If every living hero (2+) fires their ultimate within 2 s of each other, the host combines them: "TEAM ULTIMATE! / ACE + BUDDY", a big flash and shake, a camera beat on every screen (the kill-cam swing to the team's centre, for everyone within 30 m), a yellow frame burst, i-frames for 2.5 s, enemy bullets in the area wiped, then three shock rings from the team's centre (16 m + 3 m per hero past two) each dealing 26 x heroes x 1.5 "typical enemies" (`power()`; bosses x0.35), knockback, a crater, survivors frozen 1.6 s (bosses x0.15) and the team healed 30%. Each hero's own ultimate still runs. 8 s cooldown. Damage shows as "Team Ultimate" in the breakdown.
- **Emotes and voice lines.** The ping wheel has a second page: hold G / View (as before) and press **Tab, scroll the mouse wheel, or LB / RB** to flip from PINGS to EMOTES (WAVE, CHEER, THUMBS UP, LAUGH, SORRY, HELP); release on one to send it. The wheel opens on the page you used last. LB/RB and the wheel scroll don't dash, fire the special or zoom while the wheel is open. An emote is a pixel speech bubble with an 8x8 glyph over the hero ("HI!", "WOO-HOO!", "NICE ONE!", "HA HA HA!", "OOPS, SORRY!", "HELP ME!") plus a voice line: the recorded bark closest to it for Rookie, Bulwark, Dash and Ace (Let's go!, stage clear, thanks, big kill, close call), the synthesized voice for the others, panned and quieter for teammates far away; captions follow CAPTIONS. Rate-limited on the host: at least 1 s apart and at most 4 per 8 s per player.
- **Help ghosts.** When a run ends with you down at a boss (or mini-boss), your game keeps your help ghost (name, hero, colour, CUSTOMISE look, kit, team level, stage, the boss). EXTRAS > HELP GHOSTS: copy its GHOST1- share code, SAVE FILE (.json), or SHORT CODE (6 letters, kept 30 days) when the build has the online service; add a friend's code (either kind) or LOAD FILE; remove ghosts; GHOSTS IN MY RUNS on/off. Co-op teammates who fall at a boss leave their ghosts in everyone else's inbox automatically ("1 FRIEND GHOST WILL HELP IN YOUR NEXT RUN"). In your next run (solo or co-op; one per run, the oldest first), 3 s after a boss appears, or when someone is under 30% life with 12+ enemies within 8 m, a translucent cyan copy of the friend's hero appears: "MIA CAME TO HELP! / A FRIEND'S GHOST · FELL TO HIVE QUEEN ON STAGE 3". For 30 s it fights beside the hero it came for (the autopilot brain plus a 6 m leash): ghost bolts at the nearest enemy every 0.16 s (0.35 typical enemies x (1 + 1.5% x their level), bosses x0.5) and a spirit nova (4.5 m, 2.2x) when 3+ enemies crowd it. Its damage is exact (no Mods, crits or statuses) and credited to "Help Ghost". A name tag counts down; it fades with "MIA'S GHOST FADES AWAY". In co-op the host's inbox supplies it and clients draw it from the snapshot.

## Co-op / protocol (backwards compatible; peer prefix unchanged, `infinishoot-v3-`)
- Client -> host: `hello` gains `pid` (stable player id); new `emote` {k}, `cobuy` (buy back), `bye` (quitting on purpose). Old hosts ignore them; old clients still join mid-run (they ignore `cowait`/`cojoin`, so they spawn at the stage spawn point instead of next to a mate).
- Host -> clients: `cowait` {msg} (lobby status while waiting), `cojoin` {x, z, inv, back, ch [[x, z, facing, open]], ba [blown barrel i], cv [broken cover i], cs arena stage} after the usual `start` (+ `join: 1`) and `stage`; `roster` {players} to everyone else; `coghosts` {list, you} at a co-op game over; the `lobby` broadcast gains `co` {pool, join, roles, names}.
- Snapshot field `co` (generic snapCodec path; unchanged parts cost nothing): `j` [code, players] while joinable, `ai` [[hero, s left]], `pl` [lives, cost], `bb` [wait 0..1, can buy] for a downed receiver, `g` the ghost [name, hero, colour, look, x, z, ax, az, t 0..1], `e` events since that client's last snapshot ([seq, kind, a, b, c]: em emote (hero, emote), tu team ultimate (x*100, z*100, hero mask), bb bought back (hero, cost), gh ghost). Typical cost: 0 bytes when nothing changes; the ghost adds ~40 bytes per snapshot while it's out.
- The client's snapshot decoder is reset on every `start`, so rejoining in the same tab doesn't decode against an old connection's baselines; the host drops a reclaimed hero's encoder (`resetSnapFor`) so its next snapshot is a keyframe.
- Optional worker: server/leaderboard/ghost.js (`POST /ghost` -> {code}, `GET /ghost?code=` -> {ghost}; 2 KB cap, 10 uploads per IP per hour, 30-day TTL), registered in routes.js; client in src/net/ghostShare.ts (only with VITE_LEADERBOARD_URL). Offline: GHOST1- codes (base64url JSON + 2 checksum bytes, ~150 chars) and .json files.

## Unfinished / risky
- **Not tested between real machines.** Real latency, a join while another client is mid-boss-intro, PeerJS reconnection timing (how long a dropped browser takes to notice and re-host its peer) and a rejoin from a different browser (no `pid` match, falls back to the name) were not exercised.
- **Old clients joining mid-run** spawn at the stage spawn spot (they ignore `cojoin`) and don't get the chest/barrel/cover catch-up; their client-side collision may then disagree with blown barrels or broken cover until the next stage.
- **The joiner's catch-up** covers chests, barrels and cover. Other one-off state still arrives only through later snapshots (arena toys and world events are in the snapshot; scars, craters and footprints from before the join are not replayed; they're cosmetic).
- **The autopilot hero** uses the shared autopilot brain (`Autopilot.decide` with `game.local` swapped for the call). It doesn't dash, parry or cash in combos (those are input paths of the local hero), and it moves without the ice/conveyor ground effects (remote heroes never had them on the host).
- **Fair build** takes level-ups automatically (with the team's RNG), so a joiner doesn't choose their early build; their first choices are their next level-ups. It also doesn't give Mods or relics (only gold to buy Mods at the next shop). Picks are capped at 45.
- **Pool + rewind:** a rewind doesn't give back a pool life or gold spent on a buy-back (the time lane doesn't rewind gold spent on menus either).
- **Team ultimate** needs every living hero; with 4 players that's a real coordination ask (the window is a knob). The autopilot counts as a player and fires on its own, so it can't be relied on to sync.
- **Emote voices** reuse the recorded barks; their words don't match every emote ("Thanks, I owe you one!" for THUMBS UP). The bubble shows the emote's own words.
- **Help ghosts in co-op** come from the host's inbox only; clients' inboxes are used when they play solo or host. One ghost per run.
- **The HUD panel** sits on the right between the style meter and the minimap; checked at 1280x720 (with the style meter at rank A, shots/co2_style.png) only. A very tall style feed or a large UI SCALE on a short window could make them touch.
- tests/arenaCoop.test.ts failed once in a full `npm test` run ("client mirrors broken cover and falling pillars (colliders gone)") and passed 3/3 when rerun alone; the companion lane noted the same random-arena flake.
- Nothing here was played by a person.
