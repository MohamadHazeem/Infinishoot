## Findings

All 48 native CPU cases delivered 60.0 fps with zero missed 60 Hz render slots during the five-second windows. Worst frame p99 was 16.8 ms. Across the matrix, callback CPU p95 peaked at 4.70 ms, GPU p95 at 1.92 ms and draw-call p95 at 323 (four heroes, HIGH with shadows).

All 12 throttled LOW cases cleared 30 fps. The slowest five-second average was 58.4 fps. The additional 1080p four-hero test ran for twenty seconds after four seconds of warm-up. It sustained 60.0 fps on HIGH with shadows and 52.9 fps on 4× LOW (p95 33.4 ms). A 4× CPU throttle still uses the M5 Pro GPU. Physical low-end GPUs and long thermal runs remain unmeasured.

Before shadow batching, the separate CPU profiles attributed 351 ms of a five-second four-hero HIGH/shadows run to convertToBlob. Deferred clip capture averaged 1.14 ms per frame. In the 4× LOW team profile, style recalculation averaged 4.24 ms per frame and enemy simulation averaged 1.32 ms. Clip encoding and HUD styling are outside the render lane.

Packing the 48-matrix hero overlay uniform once lets all overlay passes reuse it. In a 180-frame HIGH team sample, Matrix4.toArray calls into buffers of that size fell from 64,728 to 2,520. Across three 4× LOW team samples per build, median-of-run mean GL submission fell from 3.19 to 3.04 ms. Median fps was 56.2 before and 56.0 after. The timing spread does not establish an end-to-end fps improvement.

A repeat of the 1080p HIGH four-hero shadow fixture reached 452 draw calls at p95, exceeding the 420-call budget while still delivering 60 fps. Reusing the merged hero geometry for the sun depth pass reduced p95 to 320, 321 and 323 in three subsequent runs. At an identical pose the batched and native paths matched all 262,144 shadow-map pixels and all 230,400 scene pixels. The control with hero casters disabled changed 1,605 scene pixels.

The night fixture on HIGH rendered three fog layers and 90 instanced puddles. On LOW it used the preset’s ordinary effect reduction.

AUTO now counts sustained frames that use at least 14 ms of the 60 Hz CPU deadline as busy. In the same ten-second 1080p four-hero 4× test, the previous governor stayed on LOW for 569 frames. The revised governor dropped to POTATO after 1.43 seconds and rendered 574 frames. Its eight-second climb and failed-rung backoff remain in effect.

The cold title transferred 1.50 MB, including the HTML response. The startup targets are satisfied without changing the production chunk layout. The slow 4G title took about 8.3 seconds. Worker-originated requests before the first submitted frame were zero in all four cold-entry measurements.
