## Screenshot order

1. **The twin-stick loop.** Track a moving hero through a gap in the enemy wave while their drones cover the flank. Start at `?test&stage=2&weapons=drones:5&seed=42`.
2. **A boss players can read.** Frame the Titan's eye and both hands with the hero just outside the warning. Start at `?test&stage=4&titan&titanphase=3&titanmove=beam&god`.
3. **Online teamwork.** Capture a real co-op session with a revive or team ultimate, two visually distinct kits and visible teammate bars. Use consented display names.
4. **A different weapon fantasy.** Catch Ronin's katana at the edge of a crowd in Neon City. Start at `?test&hero=ronin&biome=neon&stage=5&god`.
5. **A change of terrain.** Capture the airborne boss over Sky Fortress with open space around its silhouette. Start at `?test&stage=7&biome=sky&bossnow&god`.
6. **A build paying off.** Record a late-run evolution clearing one side of the arena while the player aims across the other. Capture an Arabic version in the same scene for the localized store page.
